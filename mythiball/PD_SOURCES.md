# Where every character's look comes from

Every character in MythiBall is drawn from a PUBLIC DOMAIN source:
the original text, the original illustrator, classical art, folklore, or a
pre-1930 film. Screen designs that are still owned (Disney, Universal, MGM,
Warner) are not used, and the AVOID column is the list of what a redraw
must keep out. The sprites are generated by `gen_sprites_v2.py`; the SPECS
and signatures there carry the same notes at the point of drawing.

Research for this table was done character by character against the
sources named. Where a source is text only (Stoker, Shelley, Wells,
Stevenson) the look is the text's description; where an illustrator is
named, it is that illustrator's plates.
