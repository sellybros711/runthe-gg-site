Growing a Run The Tour golfer: skills and boosts

A guide to Run The Tour

Run The Tour is the Pro Tour side of the golf game. You don't swing. You build a golfer, then the rounds play out off their ratings. So getting better means better numbers. This guide covers every place those numbers come from, and how they grow over a career.

The draft: where your golfer starts

Your golfer has eight skills. Each spin of the wheel shows a real golfer. You take one of their skills into one of your empty slots. One skill per golfer, then the wheel spins again.

  • Eight picks fill the eight slots.
  • Two re-spins for the whole draft. A re-spin passes on whoever landed.
  • A Mulli-Spin is a banked extra spin. You can use one when your re-spins run out. Weekly challenges pay them.

Cards come in four tiers, set by the card's overall. Each spin rolls the tier first:

  • Legendary: 5%.
  • Epic: 25%.
  • Rare: 35%.
  • Common: 35%.

Ratings: what the eight skills do

The eight skills are Driving Distance, Driving Accuracy, Approach, Short Game, Scrambling, Bunker, Putting and Composure. They don't count the same. Your overall is a weighted mean:

  • Approach: 21%.
  • Putting: 19%.
  • Composure: 13%.
  • Driving Accuracy: 12%.
  • Driving Distance: 11%.
  • Short Game: 10%.
  • Scrambling: 8%.
  • Bunker: 6%.

There's a catch. The overall takes points off for skills that sit well below your average. One weak slot drags the whole golfer down. A balanced build beats a lopsided one with the same total.

Your overall wears a tier: Bronze, Silver from 82, Gold from 86, Elite from 90. Past 100 it's ICON. Only your own career golfer can get there.

Gear boosts: the closet

Owned gear adds points straight to your skills. It shows in your overall, on the rating web and in the sim. These slots carry boosts:

  • Headwear (only with a hat on)
  • Eyewear
  • Putter
  • Ball
  • Cleats
  • Club set

Shirts, patterns, colours, outerwear and legwear are style. They carry no boost.

Two caps hold gear in check. Gear gives at most +5 on one stat and +20 in all. Past +20, the game trims your biggest boost first. The closet's boost list shows what you're getting and where a cap bites.

The boost ladder

An item's power comes from its rarity, counted in points:

commonrareepiclegendary
headwear, eyewear, balls, cleats, putters1234
club sets1345

An item you can only earn counts as rare. Some items carry a downside too, like +4 Distance and -1 Accuracy. That's the trade-off, not a discount.

Two boosts grow on their own:

  • Club levels. Your equipped club set gets one rep for each event you play in the career. Every 5 reps is a level. Level 3 adds +1 to the set's main stat, level 6 adds +2 and level 9 adds +3.
  • Collections. Finish a collection and its stat gets +1 for good. That point sits on top of the gear caps.

Putters you build in Putt Putt Tour boost Putting here. That's the one place Putt Putt progress reaches the Pro Tour.

Tour Rep and caddies

Tour Rep comes from achievement points and challenges. It climbs a ladder of ranks from Amateur to Icon. G.O.A.T. sits above that, and it needs every Pro Tour achievement. Tour Rep needs a free account. Guests don't earn it.

Rank-ups unlock caddies. Each caddie gives a small edge, like +1 Composure, or a bonus in majors. A few are there from the start. The best unlock near the top. A caddie's always-on bonus goes straight into your ratings.

Seasons: growing and fading

A career runs up to 30 seasons. Between seasons you get the off-season. You spin the wheel again and can swap a skill for the new golfer's number.

  • Re-spins: 3 every off-season.
  • Changes: set by last season. A major, two wins or a world top 5 earns 3. A win, a top 10 money list finish or a world top 20 earns 2. A solid season earns 1. A poor one earns none.
  • Each skill can change once an off-season. An upgrade applies on the spot. A downgrade asks first.

Your golfer is in their prime through year 14. From year 15 age trims skills every off-season, and it gets steeper each year. Distance fades fastest. Composure fades slowest. Good seasons slow the fade a little. Bad ones speed it up.

In the career your stats and overall can pass 99. Gear, a caddie and coaching can each push you there. The overall tops out at 112.

Where boosts don't count

  • The Daily Challenge. Every stat stays at 99 and no gear applies.
  • Ranked Head to Head. Gear never counts. An exhibition match uses it, but it doesn't go on your record.

Tips

  • Fix the floor first. The weak-link penalty is real. A 70 hurts more than a 95 helps.
  • Draft Approach and Putting high. They're 40% of the overall between them.
  • Save re-spins for the end of the draft. With one slot left, a wrong card is a dead spin.
  • Spread your gear. Past +5 a stat, a boost does nothing. Pick gear for the stats you haven't boosted.
  • Stick with one club set. Reps only go to the set you're using. Level 9 is worth +3.
  • Chase collections you're close to. That +1 skips the caps.
  • Win for the off-season. One win buys you two changes. Use them on the skills age hits first.
