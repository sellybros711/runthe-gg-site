Run The Pitch hands you a real World Cup squad every round, from 1966 to 2026. Your XI ends up a mix of decades either way. This guide is about doing it on purpose. It covers how eras get dealt, what each one is good at, and how the sim reads the side you build. It's written for Full Team, the 11-round draft.

## How the wheel deals eras

The year spins first. Then the nation, from the teams at that tournament.

- All 16 tournaments are equally likely. Each one is 1 spin in 16.
- Inside a year, every nation has the same chance.

The fields aren't the same size, though. There are 16 teams up to 1978, 24 from 1982 to 1994, 32 from 1998 to 2022 and 48 in 2026. So any one 1970 squad turns up three times as often as any one 2026 squad.

Across a draft it works out like this:

- **1966-1978:** about 1 spin in 4.
- **1982-1994:** about 1 spin in 4.
- **1998-2022:** 7 spins in 16.
- **2026:** 1 spin in 16. That's less than once a draft on average.

So don't build your plan around 2026. You'll rarely see it.

## A rating belongs to one tournament

A player's OVR is for that World Cup. It's not his career. Plenty of players went to two or more tournaments for the same nation. For over a third of them, the gap between their best and worst tournament is 10 points or more. Their best one is more often their last than their first.

So when a famous name shows up, check the rating, not the reputation. Ask which summer it is.

## What each era is good for

Look at the best player at each position in every squad, and the eras split clearly.

- **1966-1978: keepers, and boom or bust.** 28% of these squads have a keeper rated 85 or more. No era beats that. But 39% have nobody rated 80 or more.
- **1982-1994: the most stars.** 39% of these squads have someone rated 88 or more. That's the best rate of any era.
- **1998-2022: the safe middle.** Most of your spins land here. Only 19% of squads have nobody rated 80 or more. Stars are a bit rarer: 29% have an 88.
- **2026: forwards.** Half of the 2026 squads have a forward rated 85 or more. Six of the game's 99s are 2026 forwards. But the keepers are the thinnest anywhere: 10% have an 85. No 2026 defender is rated 90.

The rule that falls out: fill each slot from the era that's strongest there. Land 2026, take a forward. Land the 60s or 70s, check the keeper first.

## How the sim reads your XI

Team OVR is the average of your eleven. Shape doesn't change it. But in a knockout match the sim also lines you up against the opponent.

- **Your defense** is your keeper at 45% and your defenders' average at 55%.
- **Your attack** is your midfielders and forwards, half and half.

Your attack goes against their defense, and theirs against yours. The better matchup gets a nudge of up to 5 points of win chance in that match. It's small. OVR still decides most games. But it's there in every knockout round.

That makes the keeper the biggest single pick in the matchup. He's 45% of a line on his own. In Team OVR he's 1 of 11. It's another reason to grab an elite keeper the moment an era offers one.

## Shape: which identity fits your mix

Your three FLEX picks set the shape. You can have up to 4 forwards, 5 defenders and 5 midfielders. The shape becomes one of four identities:

- **Attacking** (3-4-3, 4-2-4, 3-3-4): attack up 6%, defense down 5%. Forwards count for 60% of your attack.
- **Possession** (3-5-2): attack up 1%, defense up 3%. The only one that lifts both ends.
- **Defensive** (5-3-2, 5-2-3): defense up 6%, attack down 7%.
- **Balanced** (4-3-3, 4-4-2): no change.

Match the shape to where your eras landed. A 2026-heavy front line wants Attacking, since the forwards carry more weight there. An old-era keeper behind five good defenders wants Defensive. If no line stands out, 3-5-2 is never worse than Balanced in the matchup.

## Judging an era pick

- **Judge the pick against the slot.** Rate the hole you're filling, not the name. A good keeper from a thin squad beats a star forward you've got no room for.
- **Age shows on every card, even on Hard.** Players aged 28 to 33 have the highest average ratings in the game, about 72. Players 21 and under average about 67. On a blind pick, lean older.
- **Captains are nearly free.** All but one squad has one. On a side around 90, a captain adds about as much as 5 rating points on one player. The captain is the squad's top-rated player only about 3 times in 10. So a captain a few points below the best option is a fair trade.
- **Awards are rare and big.** Only 41 players carry one. About 1 spin in 12 lands a squad with an award winner. On a side around 90, one award adds about 1.8. That's like swapping a 70 for a 90.
- **Save the re-spin for a dead squad.** Full Team gives you 2 re-spins on Easy and 1 on Hard. A re-spin rolls a new year and a new nation. Use it when the squad has nothing for your open slots, not to chase 2026.
