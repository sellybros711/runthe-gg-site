Putt Putt Tour is the mini golf game inside Run The Tour. You walk a trail from hole to hole and play each one yourself. It runs in your browser on a phone or a computer. This guide covers the three things that decide how far you get: stars, lives and worlds.

## Stars: what each hole pays

Every hole has a par. Your score against it decides your stars.

- **Par:** 1 star.
- **One under par:** 2 stars.
- **Two under par:** 3 stars. On a par 3 that means an ace.

A few holes set their own score for 3 stars. When they do, the stage shows it in the top right corner before you putt. Look there first.

Your best score on a hole is the one that counts. You can go back and beat it later. A 1-star hole today can be a 3-star hole next week.

Par or better clears the hole. Clearing it opens the next hole in the world. It's a story mode, so nobody skips ahead. You open each hole by beating the one before it.

## Worlds: how stars open the next one

A world is 18 holes. That's 54 stars if you take 3 on every hole.

You don't need 54. Worlds open on a running total of stars:

- **40 stars** opens the second world.
- **80 stars** opens the third.
- Every world after that needs 40 more.

The total counts every world you've played so far. So stars you pick up in one world help open the next. If you're stuck short of a gate, go back to an earlier world. Turn a 1-star hole into a 2-star hole and the total moves.

A perfect world is never required. It would mean an ace on nearly every hole. Some luck goes into an ace, so the gate leaves room for it.

## Lives: what costs one

You start with **9 lives**. They show as three hearts. Each heart holds three lives and empties from the top down.

A life goes when:

- **You go over par.** The hole is decided the moment you've used par strokes and the ball is still out. You don't have to putt out a hole you've already lost.
- **You restart or quit after your first putt.** Before your first putt, a restart is free.

Two kinds of hole never cost a life:

- **The Daily Hole.**
- **Secret holes.**

## Lives: how they come back

Every life comes back **24 hours after you lost it**. Each one has its own clock.

So if you lose three lives at 10am, all three are back at 10am tomorrow. If you lose one at 10am and one at 4pm, they come back at 10am and 4pm.

When your hearts are empty, the screen tells you when the next life returns. You can wait it out. The Daily Hole is still open while you wait.

A **Tour Pass** doubles your lives to **18**, in six hearts. A pass is for one season. You can also buy lives back from the game. The Out of Lives screen shows what's on offer.

## Spare lives on the trail

You can find spare lives on the trails between holes. A spare goes straight into your hearts if any are empty. If your hearts are already full, the game keeps it. It shows as +1 next to your hearts and fills the next life you lose.

## The Daily Hole

The Daily Hole is one hole from the tour, dressed up for the day. Everybody gets the same one.

- It never costs a life.
- You get one scored try a day.
- Playing pays **40 coins**. Finishing under par pays **40 more**.
- It's timed. The clock runs from when the hole appears to when the ball drops.
- Signed in, your first round posts to the day's board. Fewest strokes wins. The clock breaks ties.

Playing it also keeps your Run The Tour streak going.

## Playing as a guest

You can try Putt Putt Tour without an account. A guest plays the Daily Hole and the first hole of the tour. From hole 2 on, you'll need a free account. Anything a guest earns on hole 1 is kept and paid when you sign in.

## Tips for climbing faster

- **Read the star line before you putt.** It tells you the score for each star on that hole.
- **Don't force the ace.** Par keeps your lives. A third star can wait for a replay.
- **Spend lives where stars are cheap.** Short of a gate? Replay holes where you only took 1 star.
- **Play the Daily Hole on a bad day.** It's free of lives and it still pays coins.
- **Walk the trail.** Chests, coins and spare lives sit between the holes.
