In Putt Putt Tour you don't just play holes. You walk a trail from one hole to the next, and the trail is full of things to find. Most players walk straight down the path. This guide is for the ones who wander off it.

## The list at the top of the screen

Every trail tells you what's on it. The list under the top of the screen, "On this trail", names each find with a picture. It ticks them off as you pick them up. So you always know what's still out there.

Finds don't look like coins. Each one has its own shape and stands in a column of light that shows over the trees. If you see a beam, walk to it.

## Chests

Every trail has one chest. That's where the coins are. No coin lies loose on the ground.

Walk up to a chest and it opens. The coins fly out and land around it. Walk over each one to pick it up. Every coin is worth **50**. Smash a breakable on the trail and it pays **25**.

Some chests sit in a clearing just off the path. Others hide at the edge of a tree or a rock, peeking out. When you get close, the trail says a line about it and the chest's row in the list glows.

Some chests are locked behind a fence. The key is somewhere else on the trail, off the path. Find the key and the gate opens.

A chest can surprise you. Each one rolls once, the first time it opens:

- **78%:** the usual coins.
- **15%:** a big haul. Extra coins on top, at least 300.
- **5%:** a common item you don't own yet. It goes straight to your closet.
- **2%:** a free Pro Shop pack.

The roll is fixed to your account and that trail. Reloading won't roll it again.

## Golden balls

Every trail hides a golden ball on a tee. Find all of a world's golden balls and you get **500 coins** and a named reward.

## Trinkets and putter parts

The rarest finds sit furthest off the path. They're in the same place for every player.

- **Trinkets:** three in each world. Collect a world's three for **300 coins** and a named reward.
- **Putter parts:** three special putters are built from parts found across the worlds. Each one changes how you putt:
  - **Scope Putter:** the aim line reaches four feet further.
  - **Bank Shot Putter:** the aim line bends off the walls the way the ball will.
  - **Lifeguard Putter:** once a hole, water or out of bounds costs no penalty stroke.

A built putter goes into your closet. You equip it like any other putter. The Daily Hole is played without the perks, so it stays fair.

## Spare lives

About one trail in ten hides a spare life. It goes straight into your hearts if any are empty. If they're full, it's kept and fills the next life you lose.

## Puzzle gyms

Each world has two puzzle gyms on its trails. Every world has its own kind of puzzle. You might push barrels in one world and flip light switches in another. Later worlds bring laser mazes, rail yards and ice slides.

A gym is a room on the trail. A panel beside the board shows your goal, a move counter, and Undo, Restart and Leave buttons. Nothing covers the board.

Every gym has a person in it who'll talk to you. They give hints but never the answer. Every gym is proven solvable, and none can be solved by blind trying. Think it through.

Solving a gym opens its vault. You get coins, the gym's badge and a free pack:

- **The first gym in a world** pays a Pro Shop pack.
- **The second gym is the gatekeeper.** It pays a Tour pack.

The gatekeeper also guards the world's last hole. You have to solve it before you can play that hole. The world's first gym teaches the idea the gatekeeper tests, so solve that one properly.

A gym never costs a life. Take your time.

## Secret holes

Each world has one secret hole. It sits at the end of a second path that branches off one of that world's trails. It's never the first trail. It's on a different trail in every world, so you can't guess the pattern.

The local on that trail will tell you there's a secret hole nearby. They won't tell you where. Look for a path that leads somewhere: a ship, a cave, a shed. Walk into the doorway and the game offers you the hole.

What makes them worth finding:

- **They never cost a life.** Not even on a restart.
- **The first win on each pays 280 coins.**
- **They're played in their own scenery,** not the world's.
- **Beat 10 of them and you get the Diamond Putter and 1,000 coins.**

Every secret hole you find goes into the Bag, under "Secrets found". You can replay it from there any time.

## How to find more

- **Read the list first.** It tells you what the trail still holds.
- **Follow the beams.** A column of light means a find.
- **Talk to the locals.** One may mention a secret hole. Another gives a tip about the hole ahead.
- **Check behind trees and rocks.** That's where hidden chests sit.
- **Find the key before the fence.** A locked chest always has its key on the same trail.
- **Open the Bag.** It shows each world's trinkets and golden balls, so you can see what you've missed.
