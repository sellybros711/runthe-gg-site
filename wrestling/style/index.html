Icons

Stage looks

Seven rooms, one system. Every step up the business adds crowd rows, light, a screen, and finally pyro. 160 by 90 cells, seeded, so a building is the same building every time.

Motion

Sprites move in steps at fixed frame rates. UI moves in steps too: panels wipe, numbers count, impacts hit. Press a box to play it. Reduced motion turns all of it off.
