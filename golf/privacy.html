Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 3, 2026

What this covers

This page covers Run The Tour (the golf game at runthe.gg/golf). For our other games, see the main RunThe.GG privacy policy.

Playing without an account

You can build a golfer, run a season, and play the Daily Challenge without signing in. As a guest, nothing that identifies you leaves your device, your golfer, progress, and local stats are stored only in your browser's local storage. Finished guest seasons are posted to the public leaderboard anonymously (shown as "Anonymous / Guest Player", with no name, account, or identifier of any kind). Guests aren't eligible for course records.

Creating an account

If you sign up, we store the email and username you provide (or, if you use "Sign in with Google," the email Google shares with us) so we can save your career, your cosmetic choices, and post your scores to the public leaderboard. Account sign-in is handled by our database provider, Supabase, and Google sign-in is handled by Google. We don't sell your personal information.

Your username and country flag are shown publicly on the leaderboard. Your email address is never shown publicly. Note that signing in with a username (instead of email) looks up the matching account internally to authenticate you, your email is never exposed to other players through this process.

What we store about your golfer

For signed-in players, we save your golfer's name, appearance, skill build, season results, and leaderboard stats (earnings, wins, majors, overall rating) so your career and trophy case persist across visits and devices.

Purchases

Coin upgrades are optional. When you buy one, payment is processed by Stripe, our payment processor. Your card details are entered on Stripe's secure checkout and handled by Stripe — we never see or store your full card number. We keep a record of your purchase (the package, amount, and the coins credited to your account) to fulfill it and for support and accounting. See Stripe's privacy policy for how they handle payment data.

Player ratings data

The real golfers in the game are rated using statistics derived from DataGolf. This is performance data about public figures, not personal data about you, and no information about you or your play is ever shared with DataGolf.

Cookies & local storage

We use your browser's local storage to remember your in-progress golfer, preferences, and, if you're signed in, your session, so you don't have to log in every visit.

Advertising

We show ads, served by Google AdSense, to keep Run The Tour free. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet. You can opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some other third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at www.aboutads.info.

Personalized ads and your age. We only request personalized ads for signed-in users who have confirmed they are 18 or older. Guests (who provide no age) and any user under 18 are always served non-personalized ads, regardless of consent.

If you're in the European Economic Area, the UK, or Switzerland, a consent message is shown before any advertising or analytics cookies are set, and you can change your choice at any time. Residents of some US states are shown an ads privacy message with an opt-out.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how the game is used (which screens are visited, how often players return) so we can improve it. Google Analytics sets cookies and processes usage data on our behalf. Where required, these analytics cookies are covered by the same consent message described above, and you can opt out with the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Where your data is stored & international transfers

Account data is stored by our database provider, Supabase, and sign-in is handled by Supabase and Google. Depending on your location, your information may be processed in the United States or other countries where these providers operate, which may have data-protection laws different from your own. Where required, such transfers rely on appropriate safeguards (such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses).

Children

Run The Tour is not directed to children under 13. When you create an account we ask for your year of birth and do not allow accounts for anyone under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13; if you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights & choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access the personal data we hold about you, receive a copy of it (portability), correct it, delete it, object to or restrict its processing, and withdraw consent at any time. To exercise any of these, email us (see Contact below) and we'll respond within the time your law requires. You can also delete your account and all associated data at any time by emailing us.

California & other US states: we do not sell your personal information. We do use Google AdSense for personalized advertising, which may be considered "sharing" for cross-context behavioral advertising under some state laws. You can opt out via the ads privacy message shown to residents of applicable states, via Google Ads Settings, and we honor Global Privacy Control (GPC) browser signals as a valid opt-out of sharing.

Retention: we keep your account data for as long as your account exists. When you ask us to delete your account, we remove your personal data (email, username, saved career and stats). Anonymized guest leaderboard entries, which contain no identifier, may remain.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date above will reflect any changes.
