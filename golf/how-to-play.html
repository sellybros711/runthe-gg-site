How to Play Run The Tour

Build a golfer one skill at a time, then live out their career. Free to play in your browser, no download and no account needed.

The rules

  1. Each spin reveals a real golfer. Take one of their eight attributes to fill a slot, one attribute per golfer.
  2. You get two re-spins for the whole draft. A re-spin passes on whoever landed and reveals someone else.
  3. Fill all eight skills: distance, accuracy, approach, short game, scrambling, bunker, putting and composure. Spikes win events; one weak slot loses weekends.
  4. Take the best rating for an open slot, whoever lands. Cards are tiered by rating, and legendary pulls are 5% a spin.
  5. Run a season and bank prize money. Caddie, agent and tour costs run about $2.8M a year, so a mediocre build can finish in the red.
  6. Career mode runs up to 30 seasons. Your golfer ages, peaks and declines while you chase majors, cups and the world ranking.

Other ways to play

  • Daily challenge. One round at a rotating real course, same for everyone, new course at midnight ET. Post the lowest round and the course record is yours.
  • Online head to head. 1v1, Best Ball, Scramble and Free-for-All against real players. Low total wins, ties go to sudden death.
  • Pro Shop. Coins earned by playing buy cosmetics. Coins cannot be bought and have no real world cash value.
