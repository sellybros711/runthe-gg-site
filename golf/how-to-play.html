How to Play Run The Tour

Build a golfer one skill at a time, then live out their whole career — up to 30 seasons of majors, rivalries, injuries and prize money. This guide covers the draft, the eight skills, how a season is actually won or lost, the daily challenge, and the online modes.

The draft: one attribute per golfer

Run The Tour does not hand you a finished player. You build one. Each spin reveals a real golfer, and you take exactly one of their eight attributes to fill one slot on your build. Take Distance from a bomber, then Putting from somebody else, and keep going until all eight slots are full.

You get two re-spins for the entire draft. That's it. A re-spin passes on whoever landed and reveals someone new, so they are worth hoarding for the moment a genuinely elite card appears for a slot you've already filled — the most frustrating way to waste a draft is to burn both re-spins early on mild disappointment.

The eight skills

Every build needs all eight filled, and they are not equally weighted on every course:

SkillWhat it governs
DistanceHow far you hit it — turns long par 4s and reachable par 5s into scoring chances.
AccuracyFinding fairways. Carries you around tight, tree-lined venues.
ApproachIron play into greens. The single most reliable route to birdies.
Short gameChipping and pitching from around the green.
ScramblingSaving par after a missed green — how much a bad shot actually costs you.
BunkerSand play. Cheap to ignore until you draw a heavily bunkered course.
PuttingConverting. Turns good ball-striking into low numbers.
ComposureHolding up under pressure, especially on Sunday and down the stretch.

Spikes win events; one weak slot loses weekends. A build with two 95s and a 60 will win the occasional tournament and miss a lot of cuts. Which of those two outcomes you want depends entirely on which mode you're playing.

Card tiers and pull odds

Every golfer the wheel reveals sits in a tier, and the tier is earned by the card's overall rating rather than assigned by name. Aging stars have a separate PRIME VERSION card rated at their peak, which is why a legend can appear as either a Common or a Legendary depending on which version lands.

TierChance per spin
★ Legendary5%
◆ Epic25%
◇ Rare35%
Common35%

The practical rule: take the best rating available for an open slot, whoever lands. Chasing a specific famous name is how good builds get wasted.

Running a season: prize money vs costs

Once your golfer exists, you play a professional season and try to bank the most prize money you can while climbing the earnings list. What makes this more than a high-score chase is that a season has running costs.

A caddie, an agent and general tour expenses run to roughly $2.8M a year. Winnings on tour are large, but so is the overhead — which means a mediocre build can finish a full season in the red. Your net profit, not your gross earnings, is the number that tells you whether the build actually worked.

Career mode: 30 years of a life

Career mode runs your golfer across up to 30 seasons, and they age the whole way through. A young player improves, hits a peak, and then declines — so the same build is a different golfer in year 3 and year 25, and part of the game is knowing when your window is open.

Along the way you chase majors, the Atlantic Cup, the Nations Cup and international medals, climb the World Ranking, and build a rivalry with another pro who will absolutely trash-talk you in the press. Career mode also throws dilemmas at you: a wrist twinge before a big week, a swing rebuild that may or may not click, a sponsor offer, a business investment. These carry real, bounded consequences — temporary skill effects, money, confidence and reputation — and the same choice can resolve several different ways.

Finish a career at an elite level — a Career Grand Slam, five or more majors, or three Player of the Year awards — and that golfer becomes a Legend you keep permanently.

The daily challenge

One round, one famous course, the same for everybody. Eighteen holes at a rotating real venue with real pars and yardages, resetting at midnight ET.

  • Build for the course. The preview tells you which skills win there and what the day's conditions are, so you can spin a golfer designed to attack that specific test.
  • Play it your way. Pick a game plan, then choose Attack or Play Safe on the signature holes. Aggressive chases a low number and can blow up spectacularly.
  • Chase the course record. Post the lowest round and the record is yours until somebody takes it off you. The daily is human-only.
  • Legends play free. A Legend golfer from a completed elite career can replay the daily without using one of your attempts, on a separate Career Golfers leaderboard.

Online head-to-head

Draft against real people and watch the round play out shot for shot on the tracer. Low total wins, and a tie goes to sudden death until somebody wins outright.

  • Four formats: 1v1, Best Ball and Scramble (2v2 teams), and Free-for-All for four players. Everyone drafts their own golfer.
  • Quick or private: Quick Match finds you an opponent, or open a private room and share the code. A private host can open the room to the public to fill empty seats.
  • It's a fair fight. Cosmetic and gear boosts apply only in career and the daily — never online. Each format has its own win/loss leaderboard.

Coins, the Pro Shop and progress

You earn coins by playing: winning tournaments and majors, completing the daily, winning online matches and finishing seasons. Coins buy cosmetics at the Pro Shop — shirt, hat, trouser and shoe colours, patterns and club sets are direct-buy, while unique headwear, eyewear, effects, balls and clubs come from packs or from redeeming shards of the matching rarity.

Packs come in three tiers with published drop rates on the pack screen, duplicates refund coins and earn shards, and pity timers guarantee an epic-or-better and a legendary within a set number of opens. Free packs come from weekly challenges, daily streaks and referrals.

Coins cannot be bought and have no real-world cash value. Run The Tour is free to play with no purchases of any kind — every coin in the game is earned by playing it.

Achievements and Tour Rep track your progress across every mode, and daily quests and weekly challenges pay out Tour Rep, coins and free packs.

Six strategy tips

  • Fill your worst slot, not your favourite. The build is only as good as the skill you keep ignoring.
  • Save both re-spins for the back half of the draft. Early slots have the most options left; late slots have the fewest.
  • Approach and putting are the safest investments. They pay off on every course, in every mode.
  • Don't neglect composure in career mode. It decides the Sundays, and Sundays decide majors.
  • Read the daily preview before you spin. Building blind for a course that's already told you what it wants is throwing away a free advantage.
  • Watch net profit, not gross earnings. Tour costs are the difference between a career and an expensive hobby.

Common questions

Is Run The Tour free?

Yes, entirely. The site is funded by advertising. There is no paid version, no subscription and nothing in the game can be bought with real money.

Do I need an account?

No — you can play as a guest. A free RunThe.GG account saves your careers across devices, posts you to the global leaderboards, and unlocks the trophy room, achievements and Tour Rep. The same account works on every game on the site.

How long is a career?

Up to 30 seasons. You can stop whenever you like, and a finished career is what qualifies your golfer for Legend status.

Where do the golfer ratings come from?

Real golfer skill ratings are derived from DataGolf data.
