How to Play Run The Diamond

Draft an all-time MLB roster, build a contender, and chase the perfect season — the 116-win record and a World Series ring.

⚾ Play now

The twelve spots

You fill a full lineup and pitching staff: catcher, four infielders (1B, 2B, 3B, SS), three outfielders (LF, CF, RF), a designated hitter, two starting pitchers, and a closer.

The rules

  1. Spin. Each spin draws a random team-season from MLB history (1901–2025). You see that roster and draft one player who fits an open spot.
  2. Budget. You have $170M to fill all twelve spots. Prices come from WAR — superstars cost a premium, so you can't stack legends everywhere. The budget always reserves enough to fill your open positions.
  3. Re-spin. Don't like the draw? Re-spin for a rising fee ($5M, then $10M, then $15M) — up to three times.
  4. Chemistry. Teammates from the same team-season, franchise loyalists, double-play combos, real batterymates, and actual families (the Alous, the Griffeys) raise your chemistry multiplier.
  5. Roster shape. Talent isn't everything — balance between bats and arms, a strong floor, and no boom-or-bust over-reliance all make your roster play better. Your build earns an archetype like Murderers' Row or No Weak Links.
  6. Play 162. Your roster simulates a full season against real all-time opponents — the 1927 Yankees, the 2019 Astros — with a 14-game marquee gauntlet mixed in.
  7. Playoffs. Win enough and you reach October: the Division Series, Championship Series, and the World Series — and the deepest rounds get harder the weaker your team is.
  8. The chase. 116 wins ties the all-time record; 117+ makes you the greatest of all time. Every finished season is ranked among all 2,594 team-seasons in history.

How you're rated

Every roster gets a team rating from 0 to 100 — a .500 team sits near 50, a 100-win team near 80, and the 116-win record near 98 — and an all-time rank placing it among every team-season since 1901. A title isn't just about getting hot: the championship stiffens against weaker teams, so winning it all means you truly built a great club.

Achievements

Thirty-six badges span milestones, winning, the all-time list, roster craft, the roster itself, and streaks. They're derived from your finished seasons and kept in this browser, so a newly-earned badge lights up the moment you qualify.

Where the numbers come from

Player value is based on Wins Above Replacement (WAR) from Baseball-Reference, covering every season from 1901 to 2025. This is an unofficial fan project, not affiliated with or endorsed by MLB or any club.
