About Run The Diamond

Run The Diamond is a free daily-style draft game built on every MLB season from 1901 to 2025. Each spin lands on a real team-season and you take one player off that roster, twelve times, filling a full lineup and pitching staff under a $190M budget.

Where the numbers come from

Player value is Wins Above Replacement from Baseball-Reference. A player's price is set by their WAR that season, so the great years cost what they were worth and you cannot afford a star at every spot.

How a season is decided

Your roster's bats and arms set the runs you score and allow, chemistry between real teammates lifts both, and 162 games are played against actual all-time clubs. Win enough and you reach October, where the deepest rounds stiffen against weaker teams.

Is it the same game every day?

No. Every run draws its own team-seasons, so the board in front of you is new each time. The modes change the rules rather than the data.

How to play

Spin. Each spin draws a random team-season from MLB history (1901-2025). Draft one player from that roster who fits an open spot.

Budget. You have $190M for all twelve spots. Prices come from WAR, so you can't afford a star every spin. Spend wisely and don't blow it early. Scarce spots like catcher and closer are worth grabbing when a good one appears.

Positions. C, 1B, 2B, 3B, SS, LF, CF, RF, DH, two starters, and a closer. Many players fit several spots, so you choose where to play them and your DH slot is a flex.

Chemistry. Teammates who share a real bond play better together. Every bond pays both players, and a player wears the color of their strongest one:

Roster shape. A balanced roster with no weak links plays better than a pile of stars, and your build earns an archetype.

Season. You play 162 games against real all-time opponents (the '27 Yankees, the '19 Astros) with a marquee gauntlet mixed in.

Playoffs & ranking. Reach October and chase the World Series; the deepest rounds stiffen against weaker teams. Every season earns a team rating from 1 to 99. It also gets a rank against every real MLB team-season, on paper. That is not your leaderboard place.

October, batter by batter. In every mode but the quick draft and the daily, the playoffs are played out a plate appearance at a time: nine innings, real base runners, your rotation against the club's own lineup. Watch a series or sim it, and after any season you can go back and watch October from the results screen.

The record. 116 wins ties the all-time mark; 117+ makes you the Greatest of All Time, a rare feat that takes a near-perfect draft.
