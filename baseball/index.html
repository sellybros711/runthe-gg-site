How to play

Spin. Each spin draws a random team-season from MLB history (1901-2025). You see the players you can draft from that roster. Fill any open position — you choose the order.

Budget. You have $245M to fill all twelve spots. Prices are based on WAR — superstars cost a premium, so you can't stack legends everywhere.

Positions. C, 1B, 2B, 3B, SS, LF, CF, RF, DH, two starting pitchers, and a closer. Players must be eligible for the position you slot them into.

Chemistry. Teammates from the same team-season, franchise loyalists, and batterymates boost your chemistry multiplier.

Season. Your roster simulates a full 162-game season using real historical WAR. Offense is driven by your hitters, defense by your pitchers and fielders.

Playoffs. Win enough and you make the postseason: Division Series, Championship Series, and the World Series.

The record. 116 wins (2001 Mariners / 1906 Cubs) is the all-time mark. Hit 117+ and you're the Greatest of All Time.
