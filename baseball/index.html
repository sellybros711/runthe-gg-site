How to play

Draft. Twelve spins, one per roster slot. Each spin shows a random team-season from MLB history (1901-2025) and the players you can pick from it. Lock your choice or re-spin for a fee.

Budget. You have $245M to fill all twelve spots. Prices are based on WAR — superstars cost a premium, so you can't stack legends everywhere.

Chemistry. Teammates from the same team-season, franchise loyalists, double-play combos, and batterymates all boost your team's chemistry multiplier.

Season. Your roster simulates a full 162-game season using real historical stats. Offense is driven by your hitters' WAR, defense by your starters and fielders. The closer converts save situations.

Playoffs. Win enough games and you make the postseason: Wild Card, Division Series, Championship Series, and the World Series.

The record. 116 wins (2001 Mariners / 1906 Cubs) is the all-time mark. Hit 117+ and you're crowned the Greatest of All Time.
