How to play

  1. Sort all 20 names into five groups of four — every category is hidden (a team, a number, a surname, a feat).
  2. Drag or tap names into a lane. A lane only checks when it has four — lay them out and rearrange freely.
  3. Right → the lane locks and its category is revealed. Wrong → you lose a guess (and if you were one away, we'll tell you).
  4. Stuck? Reveal a group's name for the cost of one guess. Four wrong guesses ends the board.
