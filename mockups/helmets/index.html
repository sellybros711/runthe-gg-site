Mockup, nothing wired up

Pixel helmets as profile pictures

One drawing, thirty two paint jobs, and a shelf of unlockables behind it.

Nothing on this page is in either game. It lives in mockups/helmets/ so it can be looked at, argued with and thrown away without touching a thing.

The idea

A helmet is not thirty two drawings. The shell, the face opening, the cage and the place a mark sits are the same for every club; what changes is four colours, a stripe and a small logo. So the shape is drawn once, by hand, as a grid of characters, and a club is six fields. That is what makes a full league possible in an afternoon instead of a month, and what makes a retro just another row of colours.

At the sizes it would actually be used

A profile picture is small. The whole design is judged at the third one along, not the first: if it does not survive 24 pixels it does not work, however good the big one looks.

Retro and specials, as unlockables

The same six fields, different values. A retro is a real helmet a club used to wear; a special is one nobody ever wore. Locked ones are drawn and drained of colour rather than hidden, because a shelf you can see is a reason to play and an empty one is not.

It covers college too

The college game already ships colours for eighty three schools, so the same renderer gets a full set there for the cost of a stripe and a mark each.

What is honest about this mockup and what is not.
  • The shape, the stripe, the cage and the colours are real work and would ship roughly as they are.
  • The marks are not finished. Ten of them are drawn: the star, the horseshoe, the bolt, the horns, the fleur, the wing, the shield, the flag, the arrowhead and the dolphin, and Pittsburgh's three diamonds. Under every other helmet is a label saying which of the two problems it has: letter mark means the club's real mark was never going to survive nine pixels and it is wearing initials for now, and mark needs a pass means something is drawn there and it is not good enough. A leaping panther, a raven and a charging buffalo at this size are pixel art, not programming, and want somebody sitting down with them.
  • Nothing is unlockable yet. The lock states below are drawn on, not earned. What they would hang off is the badge system that already exists in both games.
  • Two club quirks the system already handles and which are worth keeping: Cincinnati's shell is a pattern rather than a colour, and Cleveland, Pittsburgh and Tampa Bay wear no stripe at all, which is a real look rather than a missing field.

If this goes ahead

The work in rough order: finish the marks, check every club's three colours against a photograph, decide what unlocks what, then a way to pick one. Storage is one short text column on the account, the same shape as the school colours the college game already keeps, and the helmet is drawn from it on the fly rather than stored as an image.
