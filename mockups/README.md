# mockups

Things that are not in a game.

Each folder here is one idea, built far enough to be judged and thrown away. Nothing in
this directory is linked from the site, listed in `sitemap.xml` or loaded by
`football/`, `cfb/`, `golf/`, `setlist/` or `wrestling/`. Every page carries
`noindex,nofollow`.

That is the whole rule: **a mockup may be deployed and must not be reachable by
accident.** It goes live because looking at something on a phone beats reading about it,
and it stays unlinked because a visitor who stumbles into it has found a promise nobody
made.

To take one to production, move the parts that survived into the game that wants them and
delete the folder. Do not import from here.

|---|---|
