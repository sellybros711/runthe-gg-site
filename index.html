What is RunThe.GG?

RunThe.GG is a free collection of quick, browser-based sports games you can play the moment the page loads. There is nothing to download and nothing to install. Pick a game, jump straight in, and play in seconds on your phone, tablet or computer. Make one free account and your trophies, streaks, saves and leaderboard rank follow you across every game on the site.

How it works

1Choose a game. Start with RunThePitch or RunTheTour above. New sports games are added over time.
2Play instantly. Everything runs in your browser. No app store, no install, no waiting around.
3Earn rewards. Sign in once to save your progress and climb the global leaderboards across every game.

The games

RunThePitch is a World Cup squad builder. Spin a nation and a year, draft a legendary player each round, assemble your dream eleven, then simulate your run through the tournament and see how far your side goes.

RunTheTour is a 30-year golf career simulation. Draft one signature skill from the greats, build your golfer, then play season after season chasing majors, the Ryder Cup and Olympic gold as your player rises, peaks and retires.

Frequently asked questions

Is there an app?

There is no app to download. RunThe.GG runs right in your browser. If you would like it to open like an app from your Home Screen, you can add it to your Home Screen in a couple of taps, no App Store required.

Is RunThe.GG free to play?

Yes. Every game on RunThe.GG is completely free. We keep the site free by showing ads, and we never charge you to play.

Do I need to download anything?

No. All of our games run right in your web browser on both desktop and mobile. There is no app to install and no sign-up wall before you can play.

Do I need an account?

No, you can play as a guest. A free account is optional and simply saves your progress and unlocks trophies, daily streaks and leaderboard rank that carry across every RunThe.GG game.

Are there ads?

Yes. RunThe.GG shows ads so the games can stay free to play. You can manage personalized advertising any time in your Google Ads Settings, and our Privacy Policy explains what we collect and why.

What games can I play right now?

Today you can play RunThePitch (a World Cup game) and RunTheTour (a golf career game), with more free sports games in development.
