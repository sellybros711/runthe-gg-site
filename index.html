What is RunThe.GG?

RunThe.GG is a collection of quick, data-driven sports sims that run right in your browser. Nothing to download, nothing to install and no sign-up wall, so pick a game and you're playing in seconds on your phone, tablet or computer. Make one free account if you like, and your trophies, streaks, saves and leaderboard rank follow you from game to game.

The games

The Perfect Season is our NFL roster draft game. A wheel gives you a year and a team, you sign one player off it, and six spins later you have a roster built under a $140M salary cap. Then you play all 17 games and, if you're good enough, the playoffs. Win everything and you finish 20-0. Read the full guide →

Perfect Season: College Football is the college edition. Same wheel, a year and a school this time, and six players signed under an $11M NIL budget out of every season since 2005. Then all 12 games, and if you finish in the top 12 of the country, the College Football Playoff. Read the full guide →

RunTheTour is our golf sim. Build a golfer one skill at a time, then play 18 at a brand-new course every day, take a rival on head-to-head or in a Scramble, or start a Career Mode of up to 30 years and chase majors as your golfer rises, peaks and finally hangs up the clubs. Read the full guide →

RunThePitch is a World Cup squad builder. Spin a nation and a year, draft a legend each round, then simulate the tournament and find out how deep your dream squad can run. Read the full guide →

FAQ

Is there an app?

There is no app to download. RunThe.GG runs right in your browser. If you would like it to open like an app from your Home Screen, you can add it to your Home Screen in a couple of taps, no App Store required.

Is RunThe.GG free to play?

Yes. Every game on RunThe.GG is free to play. We keep the site free by showing ads, and some games also offer optional in-game purchases.

Do I need to download anything?

No. All of our games run right in your web browser on both desktop and mobile. There is no app to install and no sign-up wall before you can play.

Do I need an account?

No, you can play as a guest. A free account is optional and simply saves your progress and unlocks trophies, daily streaks and leaderboard rank that carry across every RunThe.GG game.

Are there ads?

Yes. RunThe.GG shows ads so the games can stay free to play. You can manage personalized advertising any time in your Google Ads Settings, and our Privacy Policy explains what we collect and why.

What games can I play right now?

Today you can play The Perfect Season (an NFL roster draft game), Run The Arcade (daily sports puzzles), Perfect Season: College Football (the college edition), RunThePitch (a World Cup game) and RunTheTour (a golf career game), with more free sports games in development.

What kind of games are on RunThe.GG?

RunThe.GG makes free, data-driven sports games you play right in your browser. Right now that is The Perfect Season, an NFL roster draft game, Run The Arcade, a set of daily sports puzzles, Perfect Season: College Football, its college edition, RunThePitch, a World Cup squad-drafting game, and RunTheTour, a daily golf simulation and career game, with more sports on the way.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. Every RunThe.GG game works in any modern mobile browser, with no App Store or Play Store download. You can also add RunThe.GG to your Home Screen so it opens full screen like an app.
