Your privacy choices

Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information

We don't sell or share your personal information. Ads on Run The Games come from Google. They are never personalised with your data. Your browser can't change that, and neither can an account.

Some US state laws give you the right to opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information. You can do that here, and it holds if anything about our ads ever changes.

What opting out does

Every banner ad request from this browser asks Google for restricted data processing. Google then limits how it uses information from your visit. Our analytics stop sending advertising signals too. You'll still see ads.

Your choice is saved in this browser. Clear your browser data or switch devices and you'll need to set it again.

Global Privacy Control

If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, we treat it as an opt-out. You don't need to do anything here.

Your other rights

You can also ask to see the personal information we hold about you, to correct it or to delete it. We won't treat you differently for asking, or for opting out. Email RunTheGames@outlook.com and we will answer within the time your local law allows.
