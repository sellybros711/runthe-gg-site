How it works

    
    
      
  1. Pick the distance. You are going for it on 4th down.
    2. 
      
  2. Pick a formation. Use Move to line a player up where you want him.
    3. 
      
  3. Draw the play. Route: drag from a receiver. Block: drag a short bar. Handoff: tap the QB, then a back, then drag his path. Target: tap who the QB looks to first, then a checkdown.
    4. 
      
  4. Snap it. Your players run exactly what you drew. The defense picks its call every snap. You see it after.
    5. 
      
  5. Get the first down or score. Short, sacked, thrown away or incomplete, you lose.
    6. 
      
  6. Keep it. Download a play, or save it to a playbook. Playbooks need a Run The Games account.
    7. 
      
  7. Name it your way. In My terminology, say what you call a slant on the left or a slant and flat together. Every play is named from your words.
    8. 
      
  8. Test it and print it. A playbook play can be tested against every defense, then printed as cards, a call sheet or wristbands.
