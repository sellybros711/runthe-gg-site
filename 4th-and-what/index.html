How it works

  
    
    
  1. Studio. Pick a formation or a concept. Give each player a route from the list, or draw one: Route, Block, Run (drag from the man who gets the ball: that is his path), Read (the 1st, 2nd and 3rd read), Motion. Move lines a player up. Nothing else moves him.
    2. 
    
  2. Play. Set the down, the distance and the spot. Pick the defense, or let it pick. Run Play. Your players run exactly what you drew.
    3. 
    
  3. Test vs Defense. Every defense, many snaps each, and the rate for each one.
    4. 
    
  4. Playbooks. Save plays to a book on your Run The Games account. Name them in your own words.
    5. 
    
  5. Learn. Pick a position. Watch the play, then answer what your job is.
    6. 
    
  6. Print. Play cards with backs, call sheets by situation, wristbands, scout cards, practice scripts and install packets.
