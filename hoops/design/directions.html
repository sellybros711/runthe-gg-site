Career: three directions

The same screen three ways: the career hub on a phone, mid-season, with a decision on the table. The player is the real sprite from baller.js, unchanged, drawn at whole-number scales. Every other pixel here (crowd, hardwood, icons, trophy) is drawn on the same grid and lit from the same upper left. Pick one and the rest of the game is built from it.
