How a run goes six spins, one cap

The wheel gives you a club and a season. You sign one player off that roster, and you do it six times to fill a point guard, a shooting guard, a small forward, a power forward, a centre and a sixth man. Every signing comes out of a $145M cap, and the cap is the whole game: the best man on every board runs to about $300M, so it cannot be done.

Players who really did share a club, a college or a draft class build chemistry, which is worth a few points of net rating. Then you play all 82 games against real teams out of the same data, and if you finish well enough you get a run at a title.
