How to play five spins, one cap

How a run goes

The wheel gives you a team and a season. You sign one player off that roster. Do it five times: a point guard, a shooting guard, a small forward, a power forward and a center. It all comes out of a $120M cap, and the cap is the whole game. The best player on every board adds up to about $300M. You cannot have them all.

Price is what a front office would pay for that box score. Value is win shares, and that is a different number. So the board has bargains, and the bargains are the guys who won without scoring. Real teammates, college teammates and draft classmates add chemistry on top. Then you play all 82 against real teams from the same data.

Why five stars lose

The ball only bounces once

An NBA team takes about 88 shots a night. A starting five takes roughly 66 of them. Every player here brings the shots he actually took. Draft five guys who each took twenty a night and you need 100 shots that do not exist. Jordan, Harden, Bryant, Malone and O'Neal is the most talented lineup you can build here. It is also one of the worst teams in the game.

Somebody has to be able to shoot

Without spacing the paint is a parking lot. But the three-point line did not exist until 1980. Jerry West attempted zero threes in 1972 because nobody could. So shooting is measured against a player's own league, two ways: how many he took, and what share of his shots they were. Steve Kerr took only 2.9 a night. That is ordinary. They were half his shots. That is not.

An anchor, not a committee

Five guys with 0.4 blocks each are not a rim protector. One guy with two blocks is. Same for passing: if your best creator averages two assists, you have five players taking turns. Both count the best player on the roster, not the total. That is why signing Ben Wallace or Magic Johnson changes a team, and five willing helpers never do.

Then it tells you what you built

When the fifth man signs, the game names your system: Showtime, the Triangle, Grit and Grind, Pick and Roll, Twin Towers, Moreyball, Point Center and more. They are tested against real lineups. The 1996 Bulls come back running the Triangle. The 1989 Pistons come back Grit and Grind. Get it wrong and it will tell you: five volume scorers are not a system. They are Too Many Mouths.
