How to putt

Pick a ball on the course map to play that level. Press anywhere on the green, drag back like a slingshot, and release. The dotted line shows your start line, not the break. The drifting dots show which way the green falls and how steeply.

What the engine models

  • Green speed from a Stimpmeter number, so a 13 rolls out much farther than a 10.
  • Slope from a height map, which is the format a real RunTheTour green can be loaded into.
  • Cup capture by speed and line. A firm putt on the edge lips out.
  • Moving obstacles pass their momentum to the ball: windmill blades, a patrolling ghost, a timed crypt gate.
  • Six worlds of 12 levels, each with its own theme, on one winding course map. Each level adds a hazard or more slope. Hole out to open the next level; earn stars to open the next world.
  • A daily hole built from the date, so every player gets the same layout. The theme follows the calendar.
