How it works

You are down 1 with 22.0 left. Your ball, at the top of the key.

Draw the play in frames. Move, Screen, Pass and Shoot. Next frame starts where everyone ended.

Run It plays what you drew. Score late to win. Score early and they get the ball back.

The Daily is one spot a day for everybody. Practice all you want. Then take your one scored shot.

The Ladder is a run of late game spots. Win one to open the next. Stars grade the play: the win, an open look, a late shot.

My Playbooks keeps your plays on your account. Test it runs a play against every defense.

Last Shot is free. Ads pay for it. The banner sits under the court, never on it. Tap its X to close it for the visit. On a big screen two more ads stand beside the game.
