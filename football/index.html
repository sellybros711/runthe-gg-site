The Perfect
Season

Build a team out of any six seasons in NFL history, then try to win every game.

  • 1Spin the wheel and you get a random team from a random year. Sign one player off it.
  • 2Six spins fill six spots, and you have 100 million to spend.
  • 3Players who shared a team, a college, or a draft class build chemistry, which makes your whole team better.
  • 4Play all 17 games. Win 12 and you make the playoffs. Win 15 and you skip the first round.
  • 5One playoff loss ends it. Win everything and you finish 20 and 0.

Every player is scored from his real week by week stats. Prices come from what he produced, not what he was paid.
