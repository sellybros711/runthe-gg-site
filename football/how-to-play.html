How to Play The Perfect Season

Six spins, six signings, one salary cap, one season. Free to play in your browser, no download and no account needed.

The rules

  1. A wheel gives you a year, then a team from that year. Sign one player off it.
  2. Six spins fill six spots, and you have $140M to spend. Not spending it is its own mistake.
  3. Don't like the draw? Re-spin the year or the team for $5M, then $10M, then $15M, off your budget.
  4. Players who shared a team, a college or a draft class build chemistry, which lifts the whole roster.
  5. Shape matters as much as talent. A quarterback who can't throw wastes your receivers, and stars alongside nobody get found out.
  6. Play all 17 games. Win 12 and you're in the playoffs, win 15 and you skip a round. One playoff loss ends it.
  7. Win everything and you finish 20 and 0. That is the perfect season, and it is rare.

One Franchise mode

Same game with the team wheel locked to one franchise, so only the year moves and every player you sign actually wore that jersey. Each franchise has its own leaderboard. Needs a free RunThe.GG account.

Where the numbers come from

Every player is priced and scored from his real week by week statistics for the season you draft him from, so a card reflects that year rather than his whole career.

Building under the cap

  • Spend it all. The $140M is a season-long tool, not a savings account. Money left over is production you never fielded.
  • Balance beats a lone star. The sim punishes shape problems: a great quarterback with nobody to throw to, or a loaded offense stapled to a paper defense, loses games a balanced roster wins.
  • Chase chemistry on purpose. Signings who shared a team, a college or a draft class lift the whole roster. Two linked mid-priced players often beat one isolated superstar at the same total price.
  • Budget the re-spins. Passing on a draw costs $5M, then $10M, then $15M off the same cap that signs your players. One targeted re-spin can rescue a draft; three of them quietly cost you a starter.

Reading the wheel

Every card is priced and scored from that player's real week by week statistics in the drafted season, not his career reputation. That is the game's core skill: knowing which seasons were monsters. A legend in a down year is an overpriced name; a journeyman in his one outlier year is the best value on the board. When the year lands, ask what actually happened that season before you sign the famous name.

Surviving the season

You play all 17 games. Twelve wins books the playoffs, fifteen skips a round, and a single playoff loss ends the run, so the difference between a good roster and a great one usually shows in January. The perfect season is 20 and 0: seventeen wins and a clean playoff run. It is rare by design, and it is the number the leaderboards are built around.

Getting good at One Franchise

With the team wheel locked, every draw is the same franchise in a different year, so the game becomes a history exam about one club: which eras were loaded, which years to re-spin away from, which cheap seasons hide chemistry partners for your stars. Each franchise keeps its own leaderboard, which makes this the mode where deep fans of one team have a real edge. It needs a free RunThe.GG account, and your best runs carry to the boards.
