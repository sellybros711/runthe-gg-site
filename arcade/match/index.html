How to play ▼
  1. Find the five hidden groups. Every name shares a team, a jersey number, a surname, or a feat with exactly three others.
  2. Drag a name onto a rack, or tap the name then the rack. Your first group is revealed free to get you rolling.
  3. Filled a rack? Hit Check group. Nail it and it locks in; miss and it costs you a guess.
  4. Stuck? Reveal a group for one guess. Six misses and your board's done.
