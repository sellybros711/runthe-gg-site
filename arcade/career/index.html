How to play Career Path

Career Path deals well-travelled careers only: every answer suited up for at least three franchises. You get the scouting file free, with position, college and era, and then the clubs arrive one at a time, first team first. The earlier you call it, the more it pays.

  • No multiple choice by default: type the player yourself.
  • Naming them off the first club scores 5 points; off two or three clubs it is 3, and it slides down from there.
  • Stuck? Take four names instead, for a single point.
  • One wrong answer ends the run, so guess out loud and type only when it clicks.

Streaks and the daily reset

The paths refresh at midnight local time, and finishing a day keeps your streak going. It is one of the four games that come free with a RunThe.GG account, alongside Common Ground, Sportegories and Alma Mater, with one play in each per day. The Arcade Card opens all ten Arcade games with unlimited plays, the Archive of every past day, and NBA, NFL and MLB editions of five of the games.

How the trail gives it away

  • The first club is the tell. Fans remember final chapters; this game rewards remembering the opening one.
  • Cross the file with the trail: a mid-90s debut plus a small-school college eliminates half the league.
  • Well-travelled does not mean anonymous. Ring-chasing legends and late-career wanderers are regulars here.
