How to play the Number Game

Rules and tips ▾

One player, one club, one question: what number did they wear there? Type your answer and find out. An exact hit is a bullseye, and within two still counts, because being a digit out is remembering, not guessing.

  • Every correct call extends your run, and the questions keep coming.
  • You carry one save. The first real miss burns it; a second ends the run.
  • The deck opens on household names and works down into the deep cuts.
  • The daily board ranks the longest runs, so one great morning can hold up all day.

Runs, saves and the daily reset

A new deck deals at midnight local time, and your streak grows with every day you play. The Number Game is an Arcade Card game. Members play all twelve Arcade titles without limits, replay any past day from the Archive, and get NBA, NFL and MLB editions of five of the games. A free RunThe.GG account still gets a daily play of the Daily Crossword, Sportegories, Alma Mater and Career Path.

Reading a number you never memorised

  • The club in the question is the whole question. Stars change numbers between stops more often than memory admits.
  • Retired numbers are gifts. If a franchise hung it in the rafters, that is probably your answer.
  • Positions have number habits. When memory is quiet, lean on where the player lined up.
