How to play Roll Call

Rules and tips ▾

Roll Call is a daily roster memory test. You get one club and one season, ninety seconds, and a column of blanks. Every blank is a player who wore that uniform that year. Type the names you remember.

  • Full names, and spelling is forgiving: the typeahead will find him from a few letters.
  • A right player in the wrong season is not a miss. The game says so and you keep going.
  • There is no penalty for a wrong name, only the clock. Guess out loud, so to speak.

One roster a day

A new club and season arrive at midnight local time, the same one for everybody, and a finished day keeps your streak alive. Roll Call is an Arcade Card game. Members play all Arcade titles without limits and replay any past day from the Archive. A free RunThe.GG account still gets a daily play of the Daily Crossword, Sportegories, Alma Mater and Career Path.

How the blanks are chosen

Rosters run deep and most of a squad is forgettable, so the blanks are only the players a sports fan would plausibly know, drawn from real jersey records. That is why a board is eight or ten names rather than fifty three, and why naming half of one is a good day.

Getting more names

  • Start with the quarterback, the ace or the franchise guy, then work outward through the players who arrived with him.
  • Think about where the team finished that year. A playoff run pulls names back faster than a record does.
  • Deadline pickups and one-season veterans are where the last two blanks usually hide.

More recall: name the college in Alma Mater, or the player behind a career in Career Path.
