    How to play High Low

    How to play High Low

    High Low is the Arcade's endless streak game. Pick a category, then face players two at a time and make one call over and over: is the next player higher or lower on that stat? Every correct call extends the run. One miss ends it.

    • Choose your lane: NBA, NFL or MLB stat pools.
    • Each correct call reveals the real value and deals the next player.
    • There is no finish line. The run ends when you are wrong, and the only target is your best.

    An endless game with a daily heartbeat

    Your longest run is the score that matters, and the daily board resets the bragging rights every midnight local time. High Low is an Arcade Card game. Members play all ten Arcade titles without limits, replay any past day from the Archive, and get NBA, NFL and MLB editions of five of the games. A free RunThe.GG account still gets a daily play of Common Ground, Sportegories, Alma Mater and Career Path.

    Surviving longer

    • Respect the era. A 1970s scoring line and a modern one live on different planets; adjust before you call.
    • Volume stats favour long careers. When one player lasted 18 seasons and the other 9, the call is usually made for you.
    • Late in a run, slow down on the coin flips. One patient minute is worth thirty fast calls.
