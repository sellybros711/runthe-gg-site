How to play Alma Mater

Rules and tips ▾

One NBA, NFL or MLB player at a time, and one question: where did they go to college? Type the school. Spelling anxiety is handled for you: UNC, North Carolina and University of North Carolina all count as the same right answer.

  • Typing the school is worth 2 points.
  • Stuck? Take four choices instead, for 1 point.
  • One wrong school ends the run. A spelling the game does not recognise costs nothing.
  • Famous names start the run; the deeper you go, the tougher the pulls.

Recalling a campus you never visited

  • Draft-night memories are the best index. The school is usually said in the same breath as the pick.
  • Programs have types. Linemen factories, point-guard schools and quarterback pipelines are real patterns; use them.
  • When torn between a powerhouse and a mid-major, remember the great ones come from everywhere. Check the era before defaulting to the blue blood.
