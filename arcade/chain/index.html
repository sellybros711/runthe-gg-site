How to play Chain

Rules and tips ▾

Chain gives you two players and asks you to connect them through the clubs they played for. Two names go in the middle. Each one has to have been a real teammate of the name above it, and the last one has to have been a teammate of the player at the bottom.

  • Teammates means the same club in the same years, not the same club a decade apart.
  • Four wrong names and the chain breaks. The clock is the score, so a fast clean solve wins the day.
  • Any correct route counts. There is never only one.

One pair a day

A new pair arrives at midnight local time, the same two for everybody. Chain is an Arcade Card game. Members play all Arcade titles without limits and replay any past day from the Archive. A free RunThe.GG account still gets a daily play of the Daily Crossword, Sportegories, Alma Mater and Career Path.

Finding a route

  • Work from both ends. The player at the bottom has a short list of clubs too, and the middle is wherever those lists get close.
  • Journeymen are the currency of this game. A veteran who played for six teams touches half the league.
  • When you are stuck, think about the year rather than the club. Who else was there in that window?

More career trivia: Career Path hides the player behind the clubs, and Roll Call asks for a whole roster.
