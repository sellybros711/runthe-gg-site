How to Play RunThePitch

Spin a nation and a year, draft a legend from that squad, build your best XI and simulate a World Cup run. This guide explains how the draft works, exactly how Team OVR is calculated, what formations actually do, and how the match engine decides your tournament.

Drafting your squad

Every round the wheel lands on a country and a World Cup year somewhere between 1966 and 2026. You then draft one player from that exact squad into an open slot on your team.

This is where the game rewards genuine football knowledge. Great teams come from reading the spin — knowing which nations were stacked in which cycle, which squads carried a generational player, and which famous nations were surprisingly thin in a given year. The wheel is the same for everyone; what you do with Brazil 1970 versus Brazil 1990 is not.

Draft sizes and difficulty

There are two draft lengths:

  • Quick Draft — six fast rounds: GK, DEF, DEF, MID, FWD, FLEX. A quick hit when you don't want a full squad build.
  • Full Team — a complete 11-man XI over 11 rounds: one GK, three DEF, two MID, two FWD, and three FLEX slots you can fill with any outfield player. Your flex picks are what decide your formation.

And two difficulties. Easy shows overall ratings while you draft, so you can chase the strongest possible lineup. Hard hides them completely — you draft on name and gut alone, which turns the whole thing into a pure test of ball knowledge.

Team Rating (OVR), explained

Your Team OVR is the number that ranks you on the leaderboard, and it is built up in four layers:

LayerEffect
BaseThe average overall rating of your drafted players.
99-rated legendsEach one adds a little extra on top, so stacking all-time greats compounds.
CaptainsEach captain multiplies your rating up slightly — a leadership bonus.
Major awardsGolden Boot, Golden Ball and similar honours add a further boost per award.

The strongest squads push past 100 OVR. Note that this rating is pure squad quality: your formation does not change your Team OVR. Shape changes how you play, not the number you're ranked by.

Formations and tactics

In Full Team drafts, the positions you pick — especially those three FLEX slots — settle your XI into a real formation such as 4-3-3 or 5-3-2, and into one of four tactical identities. That identity shapes how your matches actually play out.

Shape is a real but secondary lever. A smart formation gives you an edge in a specific matchup and changes your scorelines. A clearly stronger squad still wins most of the time. Aiming your strength at an opponent's weakness is worth a few percent — it is not a free win.

How matches are simulated

Your opponents are real historical World Cup squads, drawn to match the round you're in. Each match is decided by a calibrated model rather than a coin flip:

  • Quality leads. The bigger your Team OVR edge, the more likely you win — but an upset is always possible.
  • The title is meant to be hard. Roughly: an 86 OVR side lifts the trophy a couple of percent of the time, 90 is about 1-in-5, 92 about 2-in-5, and a 99-rated super-team wins it upwards of 80% of the time. Below 86 it is a long shot by design.
  • Weak sides get punished early. Squads in the low 80s and below face an extra hurdle in the first knockout rounds, so they bow out early rather than fluking a deep run.
  • The group stage is a strength gate. 90+ sides go through automatically, 86+ almost always, and it scales down from there.
  • Level matches go to extra time and penalties, with formation nudging the margins.

Six strategy tips

  • Learn the stacked cycles. Knowing that a nation peaked in one specific year is worth more than knowing its most famous player.
  • Captains and awards are cheap OVR. Two players of similar rating are not equal if one of them captained his country or won a Golden Ball.
  • Chase 99s deliberately. They add on top of the average rather than just raising it, so a squad with three of them outruns a flat squad of very good players.
  • Use FLEX slots last if you can. They're your formation lever, so filling them once you can see the shape of your XI is better than committing early.
  • 86 OVR is the real threshold. Below it, the knockout hurdle makes a deep run unlikely no matter how the draw falls. That's your target before you worry about anything else.
  • Play Hard mode to actually improve. Easy mode lets you optimise; Hard mode teaches you which squads were genuinely deep.

Common questions

Is RunThePitch free?

Yes, completely free. The site is funded by advertising, and there is no paid version, subscription or paywall.

Do I need an account?

Not for a solo World Cup run. A free RunThe.GG account saves your record, streaks and titles, puts you on the global leaderboards, and is needed for the online modes. The same account works across every game on the site.

Which World Cups are included?

Squads from 1966 through 2026.

Does my formation change my leaderboard rating?

No. Team OVR is pure squad quality. Formation changes how your matches play out, not the number you're ranked by.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes — it runs in any modern mobile browser with nothing to install.
