# Run The All-Stars: improvement plan, 2026-09-02

Backlog built from `docs/audit-2026-09-02.md`. Ranked by impact on one thing: a first
time player finishing a game today and coming back tomorrow, weighed against effort.
Each pass is meant to ship on its own, in roughly two to four hours, with the tests it
needs. Passes are numbered in the order I would take them; the numbers are not
dependencies unless a pass says so.

Constraints carried from the brief: no changes to any shared RunThe.GG account, trophy,
streak or leaderboard integration; no moves to the free and paid boundary; public domain
characters only; new art stays 32x40 in the existing generator's palette and outline
style; no em or en dashes anywhere.

Testing baseline for every pass: `node scripts/check-dashes.mjs`,
`node allstars/check-posture.mjs`, the syntax check from CLAUDE.md, one headless
exhibition game to completion, and one headless season through the bracket. Passes
that touch scoring, standings, seeding or stat effects add a guard to
`allstars/check-posture.mjs` or a new `allstars/verify-rules.mjs` that replays the
situation in a headless browser.

## The passes

### Pass 1: Rules that lie (about 2 hours)

The four findings where the game tells the player something false. All in
`allstars/index.html`.

- Walk-off walk: `recordWalk` (5083) and the Phoenix path (5111) go through the same
  walk-off check hits use. Cleanest is to make `startAtBat` and `afterHitTransition`
  both end the game when `g.walkoff` is set, and to have `scoreRuns` set `g.over`.
- One tiebreak: give the game a `standingsOrder(S)` used by the hub (3339), the seeding
  (3428) and anything else that ranks clubs. Wins, then head to head where it exists,
  then run differential from the results the player has (CPU clubs carry only W-L today,
  so add a small run differential to `advanceLeague` 3204), then a stored coin flip
  saved on the season so it never changes between screens.
- Missing the playoffs: the hub banner (3273) says "Season over: you finished 6th, one
  game out." The bracket (3441) opens with the same line and a single "Simulate the
  playoffs" button that runs every CPU game with a short scoreline reveal, then goes
  to the summary.
- Mercy rule: a MERCY RULE plaque and log line at 5204, the rule stated on the settings
  screen and in How To Play. Extra innings: an EXTRA INNINGS plaque when `inning >
  innings` and the in-game line score (6816) grows a column per extra frame.
- Abandoned games: End Game (6764) in season, playoff and cup records a loss with the
  score as it stood, says so in the confirm, and the press treats it as a loss.

Tests: `allstars/verify-rules.mjs` (new), headless, replays: walk-off walk, walk-off
hit, mercy rule at 15, a tie into extra innings, a 4-3 record with a tie for fourth
(asserts hub order equals seeding order), and an abandoned season game recording a
loss. Wire it into the existing `.github/workflows` beside the posture check.

### Pass 2: The first ten minutes (about 3 hours)

- Layout: the field takes at most `calc(100vh - 260px)` on the game screen so the pitch
  name, swing modes and hint are in view with the field (line 64). On the phone, the
  SWING button and the mode row pin above the fold.
- Guided first at-bat, exhibition only, first game on this browser (a PREFS flag):
  the first pitch does not fly until the player has seen three short plaques over the
  field, one at a time, each dismissed by a click: "This ring closes as the pitch
  comes in", "Click, tap or Space when it reaches the green", "Contact, Power, Bunt
  change the ring: try Contact first". The first CPU at bat does the same for pitch,
  spot, throw. Skippable, never shown twice.
- Stat legend: one line under the draft heading (2739) and on the menu roster card:
  "POW: how far it goes · SPD: singles and steals · CON: forgives a late swing · DEF:
  wider catch and throw windows". A tooltip on each stat label carries the same words.
  The draft screen says, in bold, "Your first pick pitches every inning."
- Mode buttons get one line each on the menu (2668-2681): games, length, save.
- How To Play (7096) rewritten to the ring, the grid and the bar, with three small
  inline canvases drawn by the game's own functions so the pictures cannot go stale.
  The five second timer line goes.
- HUD wording per the audit table: balls and strikes named on desktop, "you bat" and
  "they bat" on the inning chip.

Tests: posture guard that How To Play does not contain "marker", "meter under the
field" or "five seconds"; screenshot at 1280x800 asserting `#swing-modes` is inside
the viewport at first pitch.

### Pass 3: Phone (about 2 hours)

- Park placard moves off the canvas to a strip above the line score on narrow screens
  (6638). Corner chips collapse to two: score left, inning and count right (6644-6659).
- Ball radius scales with canvas display width (6446): at least 4 CSS pixels on screen.
- Fielder scale floor on the chase and posts so nobody is under 14px tall on a phone.
- The catch ring draws a short line from the chasing fielder to the landing spot.

Tests: 390x844 screenshot with a bounding-box assertion that no two placards overlap.

### Pass 4: The pitcher moves (about 3 hours)

- `gen_sprites_v2.py`: two new poses, `windup` (arm raised, ball hand up, weight back)
  and `release` (arm forward, lead leg out), in every archetype, same 32x40 grid and
  outline rules; the signature hooks already take a `pose` argument. A `swing` pose
  (back view, bat through) replaces the procedural arc for the 240ms contact window.
- `drawRunner` (6489) maps `windup`, `release` and `swing`; `drawField` already asks
  for the first two (5996-6002).
- Regenerate `sprites.js`, splice, check the face guard still passes.

Tests: the generator's existing uniqueness guard; a headless frame capture during a
windup asserting the pitcher's canvas differs from the idle frame.

### Pass 5: It felt good, do it again (about 3 hours)

- Win: an original three-note fanfare on `Sound`, confetti on the result heading in the
  kit colour, a loss aww. Cup and season titles get a longer version and a trophy
  drawn on the season end screen.
- Player of the game card on the result screen from `pressStar` (2266): sprite, stat
  line, their `PLAYER_LINES` entry.
- Plaques promoted: green catch reads CAUGHT in hit style, green throw OUT AT FIRST,
  strikeout when pitching shows the running K count, stolen base animates the runner
  to second with the run frames.
- Milestone toasts from `perPlayer` and PROGRESS: first homer with a character, tenth
  hit, first shutout, tenth win. A small plaque at the top of the field, one at a time,
  queued behind the play's own plaque.
- Hot and cold chips: hits in the last ten at bats per character on the season, drawn
  on the at-bat card and the draft card.

Tests: unit-style checks of the streak and milestone functions in a small mjs harness;
headless assertion that a toast never overlaps a live plaque.

### Pass 6: A season you can read (about 4 hours)

- Standings (3329): playoff line after fourth, GB column, clinched and eliminated
  marks computed from games remaining, your row pinned.
- Schedule card on the hub: all seven with home or away, results filled in, next one
  highlighted, park names.
- Rivalry line on the next game card from `pressRival`.
- Season awards on the season end screen (7207): MVP, Home Run King, Speed Demon,
  Golden Glove, drawn with sprites, saved to PROGRESS. A trophy case card on the menu
  lists titles, awards and best record. Season end gets a "Keep this season" option
  that archives the summary into PROGRESS before the save is cleared.

Tests: `verify-rules.mjs` gains clinch and elimination cases (a 5-0 team with two to
play cannot be caught by a 2-3 team, and so on); awards are asserted deterministic
from a fixed season fixture.

### Pass 7: Dead air (about 2 hours)

- Between innings board: line score, next three batters, pitcher fatigue, shown for
  the `betweenHalfInnings` beat instead of a blank field.
- Windup when the player is pitching drops to 500ms after Throw It.
- Weak pitch feedback: when the CPU batter faces his weak pitch, the plaque reads
  `HE CANNOT HIT THAT` once per at bat and the batter card marks the pitch. Zone grid
  gets HIGH, LOW, IN, OUT labels.
- Bracket and cup CPU games simulate in one click with a scoreline reveal.

### Pass 8: The Cup is its own thing (about 2 hours)

- Cup screen shows the opponent's style and sharpness line like the season hub.
- Seeding by a drawn number rather than always entrant 0, so the player travels for a
  game or two; the higher seed hosts and the screen says so.
- A CPU result where the lower seed wins is called an UPSET on the bracket.
- Slide, catch and throw frames in the generator if Pass 4 went well; a runner thrown
  out at the plate is drawn arriving late.

### Pass 9: Agency and polish (about 3 hours)

- CPU bunts with a fast batter and a runner on, steals with SPD 80 plus against a
  low-CON pitcher, uses Power with two outs and nobody on.
- Send or hold: with a runner on second and a single hit, a two-second window where
  Space or a button sends him home; default is the current speed rule.
- Pixel-snapped ball, bat and ring; the four serif overlays move to the UI monospace.

### Later

- Daily character challenge seeded by date, with a menu plaque and PROGRESS tracking.
- Cosmetic unlocks drawn on the field sprite (cap trim, bat colour) for titles.
- A bullpen: second pick relieves when the starter is GASSED, for nine inning games.

## What I would start with

Pass 1 then Pass 2. Pass 1 is two hours of fixing things the game currently says that
are not true, and Pass 2 is the difference between a first-timer finding the swing and
not. After those, Pass 5 is the one most likely to bring someone back tomorrow, and
Pass 4 is the one most likely to make the game look finished.
