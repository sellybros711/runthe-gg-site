# RunTheRopes improvement plan, 2026-09-02

Backlog from `docs/audit-2026-09-02.md`, ranked by one question: does this get a
first-time player through three shows without confusion and wanting a fourth, weighed
against effort. Each pass ships on its own, in roughly 2-4 hours, with its own
verification. Finding codes (P0-1, A4, C2...) point into the audit.

Every pass ends the same way: `node --check` on the extracted script,
`node scripts/check-dashes.mjs`, `node scripts/check-cachebust.mjs`, the multi-year
invariant playtest, and a readable eight-week log of storylines, promos and the three
rooms so the result can be judged as a fan.

## The two questions that shape the order

1. **Which game?** The brief reads as a booking-sim brief. The booking sim is a v0.1
   prototype that is currently dead on the live site (P0-1) and ships trademarked names
   (P0-2). The career game is the one this session has built. The plan below invests in
   the CAREER game and treats the booking sim as a link that must not be broken. If the
   booking sim is where you want the work, passes 4 through 12 change shape completely
   and this plan needs a second version.
2. **Tests.** The career game has no test file; its regression net is the playtest
   harness I wrote this session in the scratchpad, which does not live in the repo. Pass
   1 moves that into `wrestling/verify.mjs` so every later pass can extend it. Without
   that, "add or extend tests" has nowhere to go.

### Pass 2. The first night is a scripted show
Effort: 3-4h · Impact: highest for "three shows without confusion" · Findings: A1, A5, A9, B3

- Debut booking never draws the champion, and never carries the non-title banner.
- Each indie promotion gets 2-3 more wrestlers in `world.js` (six-person rooms), each
  with a voice archetype, so weeks 1-8 are not the same three names. This is data work
  with the existing schema; no engine change.
- A "first night" script on top of the existing tour: the booker's welcome (already on
  the sheet), a one-line pre-bell scene from the interviewer segment that says what a
  good match looks like ("Hit your spots, build to a near-fall, do not go home early"),
  and a first-result overlay that points at exactly two numbers: the star rating and
  standing, and says what to do next week.
- Off week sheet: name the scene that will play ("Somebody wants a word on the way out")
  and point at the prep row ("A dark week pays double at the gym").
- Verify: walk script asserts the debut opponent holds no belt, the first result has the
  overlay, and the three-rooms text on week 2 differs from week 1.

### Pass 3. Every number gets its context
Effort: 3h · Impact: A6 is the single largest source of "TON of words" complaints, because unexplained numbers get explained with paragraphs · Findings: A6, A8, A10, A3, A4

- One helper, `withBand(value, bands)`, and a band table for OVR, reputation, standing,
  momentum, condition, mileage, freshness, respect, bad blood, purse (relative to tier
  purse range), and card position draw. Every place one of those numbers renders gets
  the band label beside it, the way the three rooms already do.
- Belt names: one function decides how a belt is written everywhere; the season chip,
  briefing and Working Toward panel all say the same name and the same kind ("the
  CDP Openweight Title, the mid-card belt").
- Card position: the booking sheet's Opener/Mid-Card/Main Event tag becomes tappable
  and shows the three inputs to `draw` with your current values ("Standing 50 · Act 78
  · Crowd 5 of 38 here. Main event at 62.").
- Codex: add entries for purse, PPV, card position and shape; every term on a hub
  screen becomes a tap target that opens its codex entry inline.
- "Where you stand" band for sub-35 becomes a rung, not a trajectory.
- Verify: a script that renders every screen and fails if any element with class
  `.val` or `.hl-v` has a numeric text and no sibling band label.

### Pass 4. What tonight did to you
Effort: 3h · Impact: consequences become visible (the core of section B) · Findings: B1, B2, C3

- Result screen adds a "What moved" strip: standing, act freshness, momentum,
  condition, and the opponent's respect and bad blood, each as before → after with an
  arrow. Data is all in `applyMatch`; it only needs capturing into `res`.
- Momentum becomes a hub meter with four bands and a toast on band change ("You are
  building"). This is the "getting over" alert.
- Inbox rows with `sev:'good'|'bad'|'story'` from THIS week render as a strip at the top
  of the hub, above the booking sheet, instead of behind the inbox button. Three rows
  max; the rest stay in the inbox.
- Verify: playtest asserts `res.moved` is populated on every match; walk script asserts
  the strip renders after a match that earned a title shot.

### Pass 5. The post-show dirt sheet and match of the night
Effort: 3-4h · Impact: the recap the brief asks for, and the biggest new feeder · Findings: C2, C7-1, C7-3, C7-4

- After Continue on a match result, a dirt-sheet card in a pundit's voice: headline,
  where your match sat on the card, one undercard line, match of the night (yours or
  theirs, with the rating), and one sentence about the crowd. Built from `PUNDITS`,
  `nightCardHTML`, `pressQuote` and the three rooms delta. Skippable with one tap.
- Three crowd-reaction lines in the live match log keyed off the crowd meter.
- Reign chip on the hub while you hold a belt: weeks, defences, the promotion's longest
  reign to beat.
- Verify: playtest counts match-of-the-night awards and asserts they are earned by the
  highest-rated match on the card; dash check on every generated headline template.

### Pass 6. Feuds you can see, and feuds that end
Effort: 4h · Impact: section E's stuck states, and the weakest-fed strong moment · Findings: B5, B6, C1, C5, E2, and the rival-retires stuck state

- `G.story` gains `kind`, `reason` and `payoffAt`. The story panel shows all three and
  the rule for the next beat ("A 64+ match against them escalates this").
- Stage 4 label only shows once `endStory` has run; a story pushed past 3 by a scene
  reads "Blow-off · booked for [PPV]".
- Rival retires or is released: the story converts to a farewell match if a PPV is
  within 4 weeks, otherwise ends with a "they walked away from it" beat and the
  Feud-of-the-Year data intact.
- A feud the office has not booked in 6 weeks gets booked: the rivalry offer bypasses
  pull once per story.
- Blow-off ceremony: result banner ("SETTLED · with respect"), moment card, media hit.
- Feud heat bar with stage marks on the story panel.
- Verify: playtest asserts no story survives its rival's retirement, no story exceeds
  20 weeks without a rivalry booking, and every resolved story has a `reason`.

### Pass 7. Promos know what stage they are in
Effort: 4h · Impact: section D · Findings: D1, D2, D3, D5

- `stakesForPromo` emits exactly one stage tag: opening, escalation, personal,
  final_warning, post_payoff. `cutsFor` prefers a matching cut and never falls to a
  generic while a stage cut exists. Write 3 cuts per stage per alignment (30 cuts).
- Event callback chips: last backstage attack, last dirty finish against you, the belt
  they took, the partner who turned. Four generators reading `G.hi`, `R.betrayed`,
  story `beats` and `reigns`.
- Alignment tags on chip and cut labels; a one-line "talking like a heel" note under a
  beat picked against billing.
- Per-beat crowd line in the promo log, from the reaction meter.
- Segment-end coherence line: "Four of five beats landed where the story is".
- Verify: a promo test that runs 200 headless segments across every stage and
  alignment and asserts no generic fallback fired where a stage cut existed, no chip
  references history the player does not have, and every generated line passes the dash
  check.

### Pass 8. Storyline templates
Effort: 4h · Impact: feuds that make narrative sense, built on Pass 6 · Findings: section E templates

- Implement title chase, betrayal turn, underdog rise, veteran vs rookie as `kind`s
  with their beat rules; faction war and injury return in a follow-up if the first four
  land. Each has a trigger, a payoff booking and an end-on-departure rule.
- Verify: playtest asserts each kind triggers at least once across 6 careers x 6 years
  and every one reaches a payoff or a conversion, never a silent expiry. Eight-week log
  printed per kind.

### Pass 9. The World and the Locker Room earn their tab
Effort: 2-3h · Impact: two data-only screens get stakes · Findings: C6

- World: "coming for you" (brewing feuds that involve you or your promotion), which
  belts you are eligible for right now and what you are missing, and your rank in each
  room.
- Crew: what a match with each person would earn, and the one closest to an alliance.
- Mentors: the first mentor you will unlock, with the rep gap, at the top.

### Pass 10. Sound
Effort: 2h · Findings: C7-8

- Crowd swell on a title change, a sting for moment cards, tiered promo pops, a tick for
  the typewriter. All synthesized in `sfx`, no assets.

### Pass 11. Off weeks and prep
Effort: 2h · Findings: B3

- Every dark week plays a scene (today: `maybeScene('any')` sometimes). The dark sheet
  becomes a choice card: the scene, or the gym at double pay, or a road trip to another
  promotion's show (watch a match, meet somebody, seed respect).

### Pass 12. Persistence and the shared account
Effort: 4h+ (needs a Supabase migration and the account layer) · Findings: A11

- Cloud save, a legacy leaderboard (legacy score), and a shareable player card through
  the same account layer the football and CFB games use. Deliberately last: it is the
  only pass that touches shared infrastructure, and the brief says not to break it.
