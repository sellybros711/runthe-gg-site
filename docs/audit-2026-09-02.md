# RunTheRopes audit, 2026-09-02

Scope: the wrestling career game at `wrestling/index.html` (live at runthe.gg/wrestling/,
noindexed, ~14,400 lines, one file by convention) and the sibling booking sim at
`wrestling/booking/index.html` (linked from the career game's home screen, ~2,150 lines).

Method: read the week loop, the match sim, the promo engine and the story engine end to
end; walked every screen headless at phone width (430px) as a fresh player and dumped the
text of each; played matches headlessly to check consequences; ran the multi-year
invariant playtest (3 careers x 6 years, 0 problems). Every finding cites the file and,
where it helps, the line.

## A note on the brief before the findings

The brief is written for a booking sim: book a show, run it, read attendance, poach
talent, coherence scoring. That vocabulary matches `wrestling/booking/`, which is a v0.1
prototype. The game this session has been building, deploying and playtesting is the
CAREER game, where you are one wrestler and the office books you. So each section below
is translated:

| Brief says | In the career game it means |
|---|---|
| book show, run show | the office hands you a booking; you prepare, then wrestle it |
| results and consequences | the result screen, then the hub the following week |
| attendance, ratings | crowd reaction, popularity, standing, purse |
| coherence scoring | there is none. The nearest things are the office directive score in promos (`complianceOf`, `index.html:11380`) and the three rooms meter |
| poaching talent | scouting offers from bigger companies (`maybeScoutingOffer`) |
| storyline participants | one story at a time, always you plus one rival (`G.story`, `index.html:6239`) |

Shared RunThe.GG account, trophy, streak and leaderboard integration: neither wrestling
game touches Supabase or the shared account layer (`grep -i supabase wrestling/` finds
nothing; the only repo-wide mention is a row in `supabase/92_ideas_board.sql`). Everything
is `localStorage`. The constraint holds trivially, and it is also finding A11 below.

The em-dash rule: `node scripts/check-dashes.mjs` is clean for `wrestling/` and this
document.

## P0: broken, or violating a stated constraint

### P0-1. The booking sim is dead on the live site, and I broke it

`wrestling/booking/index.html:481-483` loads `../roster.js`, `../corrections.js` and
`../personalities.js`. Earlier this session I deleted `roster.js` and `corrections.js`
as dead code after confirming the CAREER game never read them (commit `86293cb9`), and
that deletion went to `main`. The booking sim is the caller I did not check. Loading
`/wrestling/booking/` now logs two 404s and `TypeError: Cannot read properties of
undefined (reading 'filter')` at `renderStartScreen` (`booking/index.html:872`,
`window.RTR_ROSTER.filter`), so the promotion grid never renders and the mode cannot be
started. Confirmed headless against the current tree. The "Promotion Booking Sim" button
on the career game's home screen (`index.html:1310`) sends players straight into it.

Fix options, in order of preference: restore `roster.js` from `f5edfcb2` (the legal-name
version) and `corrections.js`, and add the booking sim to the same regression check the
career game gets; or unlink the booking sim until it is worth investing in. Either way
this needs a guard that fails when a script tag points at a file that does not exist.

### P0-2. Trademarked names in the booking sim's data

`wrestling/personalities.js` ships 40 real ring names including `Stone Cold Steve
Austin`, `The Rock`, `The Undertaker`, `Triple H`, `Karrion Kross` and `Mistico`, and
`booking/index.html:495-504` (`PROMO_META`) names the promotions `WWE`, `AEW`, `TNA`,
`AAA`, `ROH`, `NJPW`, `Stardom`, `TJPW`. The career game moved to real legal names and
invented companies for exactly this reason. The booking sim did not. The brief's
constraint ("no trademarked gimmick names... wrestlers stay keyed by real name") is
violated by the file the career game links to.

### P0-3. A championship changes hands on a disqualification or count-out

`index.html:8650`: `if(o.stakes==='title'&&res.win){ c.title=belt; ...}` and
`index.html:8670` for a defence loss. Neither checks `res.finish.type`. `commitFinish`
(`index.html:6186-6190`) lets `dq` and `count` finishes through in any stipulation that
does not set `noDQ`/`noCount`, and singles, tag, sub and grudge all allow them. So a
challenger who wins a title match by DQ takes the belt, and a champion who is counted out
loses it. Every promotion on earth has the opposite rule, and it is the rule that makes
dirty finishes a STORY beat rather than a title change. The brief lists "champion loses
but title does not change hands" as an edge case to verify; today it is the one outcome
the engine cannot produce. `pinChamp` at `:8606` already knows what a clean finish is;
the title branch needs the same test, plus a result-screen line that says "retains by
DQ" so the player understands why they did not get the belt.

### A1. There is no guided first show. There is a guided first WEEK.

The tour (`TOURS.hub`, `index.html:4694-4720`) explains the hub. The first MATCH is
explained by one coach mark (`TOURS.dec`) when the first decision fires. Nothing walks
the player through what happened after: the result screen appears cold, with a star
rating, seven numbers and a "The card they called" list. A first-time player's first
result was, in my walk: LOSS, 51/100, 1.5 stars, "Rough. The crowd drifted.", "ring
psychology too green (34 vs 40 needed)". That is honest, and it is also the moment a
new player decides whether there is a fourth week. The debut deserves a scripted night:
the booker's memo in Sabine's voice, one paragraph before the bell, and after the bell a
result screen that says what the rating means and which ONE number to care about.

### A2. Heel and face are never defined, and alignment's consequences are invisible

The create screen offers "Face (fan favourite) / Heel (villain) / Tweener"
(`index.html:1436`) and that is the entire explanation in the game. The codex entry
"Crowd & Character" mentions alignment twice in passing. Nothing says: a heel who gets
cheered is failing, a face who gets booed is turning, the crowd lean bar under the
Crowd tile is the thing that tells you which, and `alignMismatch()` (`index.html:2298`)
is quietly costing you reaction every week. A player who picked "Heel" because it sounded
fun has no way to learn that cutting a respectful promo is working against themselves.

### A3. Card position tiers are never explained

Opener, Mid-Card and Main Event appear as a tag on every booking sheet
(`renderWeek`, `index.html:5777`) and drive purse (0.6x / 1x / 1.6x, `:6092`). The codex
mentions "Opener" once ("everybody else earns their way out of the opener",
`:4831`). Nothing tells the player that position is decided by `draw` (standing 34%,
gimmick heat 22%, tier-relative reaction 44%, `:6087`), which is the single most
learnable lever in the game and the one that answers "why am I always first out".

### A4. Three different names for belts on one screen

On the week-1 hub in my walk: the season-goal chip says "Win the CDP Openweight Title"
(`seasonChip`, `:5626`, reading `SEASON_GOALS.mid_title.label`, `:2106`); the briefing
modal for the same goal says "Win the mid-card belt" (`:2112`); and the Working Toward
panel says "Win the CDP Indie Championship" (`nextGoals`, `:5614`, reading the WORLD
belt via `beltName()`). Two of those are the same belt under two names and the third is
a different belt, and nothing on the screen relates them. A new player cannot tell that
there are two titles in this company, which one they are chasing, or that "Openweight"
and "mid-card" are one thing.

### A5. Your debut opponent is the champion

On a three-wrestler roster (CDP: Pip, Ravi, August, `world.js:85-103`) the office booked
my debut against Pip Nakamura, who holds the CDP Openweight Title, and the sheet said "A
clean pin over the champion earns the shot" (`:5781`). The earlier fix that stopped
rookies debuting in ladder matches (`:6015-6023`) did not extend to opponents. Week one
should not be a non-title match with the champion, and the non-title banner should not be
the second thing a new player reads. Root cause is roster size (A9).

### A6. Numbers shown without context

Every number below appears on a first-week screen with no label, band, comparison or
"good/bad" anchor. Numbers WITH context are listed after, because the game already knows
how to do this and should do it everywhere.

Without context:
- Create: seven attribute values (32, 37+5, 30...) and the `×1.15` growth multiplier.
- Hub HUD: `OVR 34`, `POP 5`, `COINS 1,500`. What is OVR out of? Is 34 a rookie?
- Business panel: `Standing 50 · Just a body` (the band helps), `Mileage 0`, `Freshness 78, hot`, `$450/wk`, `Merch cut 6%`.
- Result: `Reputation +17`, `XP +60`, `Purse $146`, `Popularity -0.0`, `2 near-falls`, `5 moves called`, `OVR 32` on the opponent.
- Details panel: `Where you stand 27 · On the way out` (for a debuting rookie, see A8), `How hot you are 48 · Warm`, `Momentum 50`, `Condition 100`.
- Locker room: `Respect 0 · Bad blood 0` x3.
- Hall of Fame: `Legacy score 24`, `In the ring 0`, `Draw 4`, `Business 3`, `Character 50`.
- Booking details: `+21 rep`, `0% risk`, `You 57%`.

With context (keep, and copy the pattern):
- Arsenal locks: `Power 32/38 · mastery 0/70`.
- Training: `Next gate: 38 → Running Elbow`.
- Three rooms: `48 · One of the roster` with a band label and a month arrow.
- Season briefing rows: `Standing with the office 50 of 62`.
- Star rating: `51/100 · 1.5★ · Rough. The crowd drifted.`

### A7. `Popularity -0.0`

`renderMatch`, `index.html:8982`: `(v>=0?'+':'')+v.toFixed(1)` on a value of about
-0.04 prints "-0.0" in red. Cosmetic, but it is on the FIRST result screen a player sees
and it reads as "the game is broken".

### A8. "Where you stand: 27, On the way out" for a wrestler who has not debuted

`trajectoryScore` (`:1926`) blends standing 50, pop 5 and tier progress 0, and
`trajectoryBand` calls anything under 35 "On the way out". A brand-new rookie is told
they are on the way out. It lives behind the "Show every stat" toggle now, but the band
text is wrong for the situation and should read as a rung ("Bottom of the card") rather
than a trajectory.

### A9. The starting roster has three people in it

`world.js`: CDP has 3 wrestlers, GCW 4, SCW 3, Rose 2. `makeOffers` (`:6006`) draws
three offers from the house, so on the indies every week is one of the same 2-3
opponents, one of whom holds a belt. The tour promises "34 rivals to face"; the first
year delivers three. This is the root of A5, of the "Never Met x3" locker room, and of
"the week-to-week flow does not make sense" as a feeling: nothing new walks through the
door.

### A10. Management-sim terms used before they are defined

Standing, reputation (rep), popularity, momentum, mileage, freshness, downside,
purse, mastery, tier, gate, respect, bad blood, pull, directive, and "going over" all
appear on week-one screens. The codex (`{n:'The Week'}` through `{n:'The Climb'}`,
`index.html:4820-4917`, 1,408 words) defines standing (7 mentions), reputation (4),
XP (4), respect (4), turn (6), and mentions momentum, mileage, freshness, gimmick,
condition and blow-off exactly once each. It never uses the words purse, OVR, PPV,
Mid-Card, card position, or shape. The codex is also only reachable via "? HELP" and the
last tour step says so; nothing on the screen where a term appears links to its
definition.

### A11. No account, no cloud save, no trophies

Everything lives in `localStorage` (`save()`, `index.html`). A career is lost with a
cleared browser. The other RunThe.GG games carry accounts, streaks and leaderboards
through Supabase (`supabase/10_accounts.sql` onward); the wrestling game has none of it.
Not a first-week problem, but it is the reason "wanting a fourth show" has no external
hook (no streak, no legacy leaderboard, no shareable card beyond the local player card).

## B. Scene and situation coherence

### The trace: booked, played, paid, next week

1. `advanceWeek()` (`index.html:9241`) clears offers, booking, prep; applies the road
   (condition, momentum, wear, standing drift, pay, gimmick decay); rolls reaction toward
   fame; heals; loyalty; betrayal check; contract; scouting; year end; `worldTick()`;
   banks the three-rooms delta.
2. `renderCareer()` (`:5330`) calls `renderWeek()` which calls `bookWeek()` (`:8292`):
   tour, tournament, then a roll for match (~67-86%), promo (~7-16%) or dark week.
3. Match: `makeOffers()` (`:5973`) builds three offers; `bookWeek` ranks them by worth
   and hands the player the one their PULL allows (`:8327-8334`).
4. `goBooking()` plays a backstage segment or scene on the way, then `startFight`.
5. `finalizeFight()` (`:8178`) computes quality, calls `applyMatch()` (`:8556`), then
   `renderMatch()` (`:8967`) and `go('match')`.
6. Continue returns to the hub. Nothing advances the week until the player leaves the
   result: `advanceWeek` is called from `pmDone` / `endDarkWeek` / the fight's continue.

Where consequences are invisible or unexplained:

### B1. The result screen shows what you earned, not what changed

`renderMatch` shows purse, rep, pop, XP, stars, the story sentence and good/bad lists.
It does NOT show: standing moved (`applyMatch` `:8568`, up to +/-6 a night), gimmick heat
moved (`:8572`), momentum moved (`:8589`), condition spent (`:8590`), reaction moved
(`:8587`), respect/heat with the opponent moved (`:8800-8808`), or the three rooms.
Every one of those is the answer to "what did tonight do to me", and every one of them
is silent until the player finds the tile next week and notices the number is different.
The press quote (`pressQuote`) and dirt-sheet card exist on this screen and are good,
but they talk about the match, not about the career.

### B2. Consequences arrive as inbox rows, and the inbox is behind a button

`pushInboxItem` is how the game reports title shots earned (`:8626`), rematch clauses
(`:8648`), stale acts (`:9285`), contract expiry, feud starts (`:6248`), feud shape
(`:6258`), scouting, betrayals. The inbox opens from a small "Full inbox & news" button
in the Business panel (`:5903`) and shows three rows in the story panel. In my walk the
hub at week 1 showed no inbox rows at all above the fold on a phone. A new player will
never open it.

### B3. Off weeks are a third of the year and have one button

`bookWeek` rolls dark for roughly 25-30% of weeks for a rookie (pMatch ~0.67, pPromo
~0.07, `:8316-8321`). (An earlier draft of this finding cited 17 matches in 40 weeks
from the playtest; that harness was advancing the week twice after every match, since
`applyMatch` already ends the week. The pMatch arithmetic stands; the count did not.)
The dark
sheet (`:5804-5807`) is a sentence and "Call It A Week". `endDarkWeek` (`:8399`) tries
`maybeScene('any')`, so some off weeks get a scene, but the sheet itself does not say so
and the player is presented with a week where the only action is to skip it. The prep
row still works on a dark week and pays double (gym +55 XP); nothing on the sheet points
at it.

### B4. Modals persist across screen changes

`modal()` (`:2699`) is one global overlay. In the walk the season briefing, fired 420ms
after the tour ended (`:4739-4741`), was still open when the match result rendered
behind it. The result screen was unreadable until "Got it". Any `go()` should close a
plain modal, or the briefing should not fire until the player is idle on the hub.

### B5. Story stage 4 is labelled "Settled" while the feud is live

`advanceStory` (`:6261`) is `Math.min(4, stage+1)` and is called from scenes (`:14071`,
`:14159`), the hot mic (`:12174`), backstage (`:6366`), and promos. A scene at stage 3
pushes the feud to stage 4 without a match. The story panel then reads `STORY_STAGES[4]`
= "Settled · Whatever it was, it is finished." (`:6219`) while `G.story` is still set,
the rival still headlines your offers, and the blow-off has not happened. The player
sees "finished" on a feud that is not.

### Edge cases the brief asked about

| Case | Career game | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Champion loses but title does not change hands | Cannot happen. DQ/count-out wins take the belt (P0-3). | broken |
| Wrestler booked twice on one card | You wrestle once. Undercards from `pickUndercardPairs` (`:8889`) use a `used` set, so no duplicates; your opponent is excluded. PPV card (`:5761`) same. | fine |
| Feud partner injured | CPU injuries (`:4559`) exclude the rival explicitly (`anyRoster([c.rivalId])`), so a rival is never injured mid-feud. Convenient, but it means "my rival got hurt and the feud is on hold" never happens as a story. | safe, flat |
| Feud partner gone (defects) | Handled: `:4550-4555` clears rival and story with an inbox line. | fine |
| Feud partner gone (retires or released) | NOT handled. `w.retired[...]` is set at `:4336` (release) and `:4373` (retirement) with no check of `c.rivalId` or `G.story`. The story keeps pointing at a retired wrestler; `houseRoster` filters them out so the feud can never be booked or ended. Stuck story until the player jumps companies. | stuck state |
| Promo by someone not in a storyline | Promo target is `promoTargets()[0]` (`:8336`); the office sends you to talk about whoever is worth it. Fine for a career game: you are always in your own segment. | fine |
| Storyline ending without a payoff | `endStory` is only called from the blow-off match (`:8785`), the truce option (`:6342`), and a scene. Stage can reach 4 without either (B5). A feud with a rival the player never gets booked against (pull too low, A-side of B6) has no expiry: `R.cooldown` only exists after resolution. | can run forever |
| Two storylines contradicting on one show | One story at a time by design (`G.story` is a single object). `brewingFeuds` (`:4597`) are news-only between CPU wrestlers. No contradiction possible, and no second story possible either. | n/a |

### B6. Your own feud can sit unbooked

`bookWeek` sorts the three offers by worth (rivalry = 70 + Main Event 40) and gives a
player with pull 0 the LAST one (`:8327-8334`), which is never the feud. At pull 1 it is
the feud roughly a third of the time. Rivalry matches did get booked in the playtest
(8-18 across six years, `/tmp/playtest/deep.txt`), so the gate is porous, but a rookie's
first feud (started by a promo at `:12138` or by heat at `:8828`) can go weeks with the
rival headlining a card the player is not on. The story panel says "Escalation" while
nothing escalates.

### C1. Feuds are the weakest-fed strong moment in the game

A feud is the spine of a wrestling career and every beat of it is an inbox row. Start,
each escalation, the shape being set, and the blow-off all land as `pushInboxItem`.
Compare a title change, which gets a banner, a scene, a media hit and a moment card. The
blow-off should be at least a title change's equal: banner on the result ("SETTLED ·
with respect" / "in blood"), a moment card, a media hit from Brandt or Shaw, and a line in
the year-end ceremony (the "Feud of the Year" badge exists at `:9985` but only shows in
the awards ribbon).

### C2. No post-show recap exists

The result screen has `pressQuote` (one line) and the news feed carries CPU events.
There is no "last night" write-up: what the show was, where your match sat on it, what
the undercard did, who got over. `nightCardHTML` (`:8922`) already assembles an
undercard with ratings, which is most of a recap. The booking sim has a working
template for exactly this (`generateColumn`, `booking/index.html:1925`).

### C3. Momentum exists and is invisible

`c.mom` moves every match (`:8589`) and every week (`:9271`), feeds win probability
(`:8542`) and heat score, and appears only inside the "Show every stat" panel as a bare
number. A "getting over" alert is the single most requested feeder in the brief and the
data for it is already there.

### C4. Reign counters are on the record screen only

`c.defenses` shows on the And STILL banner and in nextGoals. There is no live "Champion
· 6 weeks · 3 defences" chip on the hub while you hold a belt; the notesRow (`:5355`)
shows the belt name only.

### C5. Feud intensity is a stage label, not a meter

The story panel shows the stage name and "Bad blood 48 · 5-1 against them". Heat is
0-100 and rises every meeting; showing it as a climbing bar with the stage boundaries
marked (Spark / Escalation / Personal / Blow-off) makes escalation visible.

### C6. Screens that are pure data with no payoff

- The World tab: nine promotions of rosters and champions. Nothing happens here; nothing
  you tap changes anything except opening a profile. Stakes to inject: "who is coming
  for you" (brewingFeuds already exist, `:4597`), which champions you are eligible to
  challenge, and where you rank in each room.
- The Locker Room (crew) at low respect: a list of names with two zeros each and a rule.
  Payoff to inject: the respect a match with each person would earn, and a single
  "closest to an alliance" callout.
- The Record screen before year two: eight zeros. Fine as an empty state.
- Mentors at 0 rep: twelve locked cards. Should show the one you will unlock first and
  how far away it is, the way Arsenal does.

### C7. Proposed new feeders (fit the genre and the existing engine)

1. Post-show dirt sheet: a half-screen card after Continue, in a pundit's voice, with a
   headline, where your match sat, the undercard line, and "match of the night"
   (yours or somebody else's). Uses `PUNDITS`, `nightCardHTML`, `pressQuote`.
2. "Getting over" meter and alert: momentum as a bar on the hub with a one-line state

3. Crowd reaction text during the match: the crowd meter already exists; add three
   lines in the log per match keyed off it ("The building came alive on the second

4. Reign chip: "Champion · week 6 · 3 defences · longest reign in CDP: 22 weeks".
5. Feud heat bar with stage marks (C5), and a blow-off ceremony (C1).
6. Moment of the year tracker: a running "best so far" on the hub with the current

7. Streak rewards: three well-received matches (70+) in a row unlocks a named streak
   ("Hot Hand") with a purse multiplier the booking sheet shows.
8. Sound: a crowd swell on a title change, a distinct sting for a moment card, a promo

## D. Promos

### How a promo is generated

`beginPromo` (`index.html:11541`) builds `PMCTX` from `buildPromoCtx` (`:10490`): you,
the target, shared history, the belt picture, the feud stage, the upcoming match and the
office directive. The player then plays five beats (`PPHASE_ORDER`, `:11267`: hook,
thesis, meat, personal, close) in one of three modes: Skip, Quick Cut (`cutsFor`,
`:11268`, three authored cuts from `CUT_BANK`, 24 entries), or Build (`chipsFor`,
`:11156`, up to six chips from `CHIP_BANK`, 65 entries). Every chip and cut is gated by
`align`, `style`, `gimmick`, `stakes` tags and a `when(ctx)` predicate. The opponent
answers each beat (`promoReply`, `:11465`) from their own `lines` 55% of the time or a
style bank (`REPLY_BANK`, five styles). The office directive (`DIRECTIVES`, `:11316`)
is picked by the promotion's named booker (`BOOKERS`, `:11329`). Rating (`pmFinish`,
`:12102`) blends beat scores, charisma, specifics, crowd work and structure, and pays
pop, gimmick heat, rep, standing and XP.

This is a real promo engine, not a random line picker. The criticisms below are about
coverage and visibility, not architecture.

### D1. Stage awareness exists as tags, and six cuts use them

`stakesForPromo` (`:11136`) emits `feud`, `feud_early` (stage 0-1) and `feud_end`
(stage 3+). Nothing emits a tag for stage 2 (Personal), which is the beat the brief calls
out. `CUT_BANK` has 6 of 24 entries tagged for a feud at all. So the "personal" stage of
a feud reads the same as the "opening shot" stage in Quick Cut most of the time, and
Build mode's chips (65) lean on history ("you beat me in Osaka") rather than on stage.

### D2. Alignment contradictions are allowed, silently

`promoKinds` (`:11295`) filters by phase and situation but not by alignment: a face can
pick MOCK, THREATEN or CHEAP HEAT in any beat. The game treats that as a turn in
progress (`turnProgress`, `:2348`) which is a good design, but the player is not told
that is what they are doing until `weekAdvice` says "Talking like a heel. One more promo
finishes the turn." The chip labels carry no alignment cue, so a first-timer who picked
Face and then picks three threats has turned heel without knowing the word.

### D3. Callbacks exist for wins and losses, not for the events that hurt

`CHIP_BANK` references: previous wins/losses, best match, venue, streak, belt, title
count. No chip or cut references: an attack (the backstage jump at `:6321`), a cost
match (`:6333`), a screwjob, an injury they gave you, a partner's betrayal, a title you
lost to them. Those are the moments a real promo is ABOUT, and the game has all of them
in `G.hi`, `c.storiesResolved`, `R.betrayed` and the story `beats`.

### D4. Generic promos are the fallback in three places

`promoReply` falls to "We will see you out there." (`:11496`) when a character's pools
are empty for the mode. `PROMO_LINES` (`:11194`) is the legacy pool behind Quick Cut's
fallback; its lines are voice-neutral ("${o}, you and me. No excuses left."). And the
office booking `why` line for a promo defaults to "They want you on the microphone."
(`:8337`). None of these are wrong, but they are the lines a player will see most on a
three-person roster.

### D5. Crowd reaction is a number after the fact

`pmMeter` updates REACTION and the lean dot during the segment (`:11508`), and
`pmCall` shouts one line at the end. There is no per-beat crowd response text ("Boos.
Real ones." / "A pop that surprised even you.") so the middle of the promo is silent.

### Proposed promo structure

Keep the five-beat form and the three modes. Add three things:

1. A STAGE dimension on every cut and chip: `opening` (no history, first meeting),
   `escalation` (feud stage 1, or a rematch), `personal` (feud stage 2, or any
   betrayal/screwjob/injury in shared history), `final_warning` (stage 3, blow-off
   booked), `post_payoff` (a resolved story with this person). `stakesForPromo` emits
   exactly one stage tag; `cutsFor` prefers cuts that match it; a feud with no matching
   cut falls through to the closest stage, never to generic.
2. An ALIGNMENT frame on the chip labels: heel chips carry a small red "HEEL" tag, face
   chips a cream "FACE" tag, cool ones neither. Picking against your billing shows a
   one-line "You are talking like a heel" under the beat. The turn stays possible and
   becomes a choice.
3. CALLBACK chips generated from events, not just results: the last backstage attack,
   the last dirty finish against you, the belt they took, the partner who turned. Each
   reads one line from the record and names the week.

Quality feeding momentum and coherence: `pmFinish` already pays pop, heat, rep,
standing. Add: momentum (+/-6 by rating band), and a COHERENCE read that is visible: the
segment-end line states which of the five beats fit the stage and alignment ("Four of
five beats landed where the story is") so the player sees why 62 and not 80.

## E. Storylines

### What a storyline is today

One `G.story` object (`:6245`): `charId`, `stage` 0-4, `beats`, `events`,
`startWeek/Year`, `shape` (aggressive / cool / cerebral / underdog, set by the first
meaningful action). Started by heat (two matches, heat 42+, `:8828`), by a promo
(`:12138`), by a scene, by a defining rival returning (`:9249`). Advanced by a 64+ match
or a rivalry-stakes match, a scene, a backstage choice, a hot-mic interruption. Ended by
the stage-3+ match (`:8785`, respect if quality 72+ or a win with 40+ respect, else
blood), the truce, or a scene. Stipulation ladder by shape (`RIV_STIP_BY_SHAPE`,
`:6228`). Recorded to `storiesResolved` with average quality for awards and HOF.

Against the brief's checklist:

| Requirement | Status |
|---|---|
| Participants | you + one rival. Never a third party, a partner, a faction |
| A reason it started | `startStory(charId, reason)` stores the reason in the log and inbox only. `G.story` does not keep it, so nothing later can reference it |
| A current stage | yes, five named stages |
| A planned payoff | implicit: a stage-3 match ends it. Not shown to the player as "this ends at [PPV]" |
| A way to end | blow-off, truce, defection, company jump. NOT retirement or release of the rival (B: stuck state) |
| Can run forever | yes: no expiry, no cooling. A feud whose rival the office never books you against sits at Escalation indefinitely |
| Never references its origin | correct, it cannot (reason is not stored) |
| Ignores results | partly. Advancement needs a 64+ match, which a rookie against a better rival may never hit, so a face who "keeps losing clean" does not escalate and does not resolve. The underdog shape exists for exactly that case but only triggers on the FIRST match |
| Contradicts alignment | not checked. A face-vs-face or heel-vs-heel feud has the same beats and shapes as face-vs-heel. `charLean` (`:2360`) knows the room's opinion of the rival; the story does not use it |

### E1. Coherence has no score and no feedback

There is no coherence number in the career game. The promo directive score (`comply`)
is the only "did that make sense" signal, and it is printed once as "office directive
delivered / partial / ignored" (`:12131`). The brief's test ("if the player cannot tell
why coherence dropped, that is a finding") applies to STANDING, which is the nearest
thing: it moves for match quality, directive compliance, working hurt, refused bookings,
season goals and injuries, and the player only ever sees the total.

### E2. The feud panel does not say what to do next

The story panel (`renderStoryPanel`, `:5432`) shows the stage label and "BLOW-OFF
AVAILABLE" at stage 3. It never says "win a 64+ match against them to escalate", "the
office books the blow-off at [PPV]", or "they are on the other brand until the
supershow". Escalation feels random because its rule is hidden.

### Proposed storyline templates

Each template has: participants, a trigger, beats with a rule per beat, a booked payoff,
and end conditions including the rival leaving. All can be built on `G.story` by adding
`kind`, `reason` and `payoffAt` fields.

| Template | Trigger | Beats (each is a week the office can book) | Payoff | Ends if |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Title chase | you pin the champion clean, or call for the belt at 60+ reaction | 1 non-title win · 2 promo face-off · 3 contract signing scene · 4 title match at the next PPV | title match, win or lose | belt changes hands to a third party (feud re-targets), rival leaves |
| Betrayal turn | ally loyalty < 25, or you turn on them | 1 the turn (scene) · 2 they cost you a match · 3 grudge match · 4 blow-off at PPV, respect or blood | blow-off | rival leaves; a truce scene |
| Underdog rise | first match vs a rival 10+ OVR above you is a loss | 1 loss · 2 near-miss (draw or 70+ loss) · 3 upset win · 4 rubber match at PPV | rubber match | you outgrow them (OVR passes theirs by 8): story converts to "rivals as equals" |
| Veteran vs rookie | a 34+ opponent with 20+ respect for you, or you are the veteran vs a debut | 1 lesson (loss, respect up) · 2 the rookie steals a pin · 3 the veteran's last stand · 4 handshake or hard feelings | stage-4 match | veteran retires (a farewell match becomes the payoff, not a dead end) |
| Faction war | you join or found a stable and a rival faction exists | 1 numbers game costs you a match · 2 tag match · 3 leader vs leader · 4 war games or a cage at PPV | stage-4 match | you leave the faction; either faction dissolves |
| Injury return | you return from 6+ weeks out, and the injury came from a named opponent | 1 the return promo · 2 they target the injury (limb work) · 3 you work hurt and win, or lose again · 4 blow-off | blow-off | rival leaves |

Every template writes a `reason` and `payoffAt` the panel can show ("Started when Ravi
Chandra cost you the Openweight Title, Y1 W14. Ends at Best of the Best, W20.") and every
template converts rather than dies when the rival retires or leaves.

## Appendix: the booking sim, briefly

State: v0.1 (`booking/index.html:489`), noindexed, one file, 135 functions, its own
save. It has the shape the brief describes (build a card, run it, a graded scorecard
with crowd / TV / attendance / gate / net, feuds with heat and blow-off, a dirt-sheet
column, market and poaching, contracts and morale). Beyond P0-1 and P0-2:

- Onboarding: one help mention in 2,145 lines. The start screen is two mode cards and a
  promotion grid with no explanation of what a segment, intent, or feud is.
- `calcSegmentRating` (`:1182`) is fully inspectable (every modifier is a note); that is
  the best "why did the rating do that" design in either game and the career game could
  borrow it for standing.
- `generatePromo` (`:1939`) is three heat bands of one or two templated lines. Every
  promo is generic, per the brief's definition.
- Feuds: created by hand, heat up per booked match, blow-off flag at 80, cooling at -3 a
  week. No stages, no reason, no payoff other than a PPV match at 70+.
- Wrestler booked twice on one card: `addSegment` (`:1600`) does not check the rest of
  the card. Allowed.
- Title on DQ: no finish types exist; a winner is chosen by the player. n/a.

Whether to invest here is the first question the plan asks.
