# Run The All-Stars: improvement audit, 2026-09-02

Scope: `allstars/index.html` (the whole game, 7,260 lines), `allstars/gen_sprites_v2.py`
(the sprite generator), `allstars/check-posture.mjs`. Every screen was walked headless
at 1280x800 and 390x844 with a fresh browser profile, one exhibition game was driven
to completion, a season was started, resumed, fast-forwarded to the bracket, and a cup
was entered. Numbers below that are not line references come from small simulations
of the game's own formulas, noted where they appear.

Line numbers refer to `allstars/index.html` unless another file is named.

## A. First-time user understanding

Walked as someone who has never seen the game.

### Menu (2658)

- "Choose a mode" explains Exhibition and Season in the intro paragraph and never
  mentions the Cup, which is the third button (2661-2665). The buttons carry no
  descriptions of length, stakes or save behaviour.
- The 54-card roster wall below the buttons shows POW SPD CON DEF numbers with no
  legend. A first-timer reads them as decoration. Nothing on this screen or the draft
  screen says what a stat does in play.
- Do I know what to do next: yes, the red buttons are clear. Do I know why it matters:
  no. Nothing says a season is about 90 minutes and a cup is about 40.

### How To Play (7096)

- Batting section (7103-7110) describes "a marker sweeps left to right across the
  meter under the field" with a green zone and yellow band. The game replaced that with
  a shrinking ring on the field (`drawTimingRing` 6546). The `#meter` CSS at 217 is for
  an element `renderGame` no longer creates. A player who reads the help before playing
  is taught to look at the wrong place.
- Pitching section says "No pick within five seconds and the pitcher shakes off the
  sign" (7135-7137). The timer was removed; the pitcher waits. The in-game strip is
  correct, the help is stale.
- The help never says the first pick is your pitcher for the whole game, which is the
  draft rule that decides most of what a new player experiences on defense.
- The help is text only. Nothing shows the ring, the grid or the throw bar.

### Draft (2735)

- "Choose nine characters. First pick bats first and pitches." is the only guidance.
  No suggested lineup, no "what makes a good team", no stat legend. Randomize exists
  and auto-advances (good) but does not say it is the quick start.
- Role labels are one word (Power, Speed, Contact, Wild, Balanced, Defense, Underdog).
  Wild is used for eleven characters and does not tell the player anything about how the
  character plays.
- Locked cards say exactly how to earn them (2762). Good. They do not show progress
  toward it (3 of 6 homers).

### Franchise (2526)

- Reads well. Park cards state their factors in plain numbers. The one gap: nothing on
  this screen says how many games a season is or where the schedule sits.

### Settings (2863)

- Innings and difficulty have no consequences stated. "5 innings" against "9 innings"
  should say roughly how long each takes and that 9 innings unlocks Father Time.
- Game speed copy is good.

### The game screen (6615)

- At 1280x800 the field takes the whole viewport (body.ingame #field max-height 80vh,
  line 64). The pitch name, the swing mode buttons, the "click anywhere or press Space"
  line and the ring hint are all under the fold. Screenshot: the first pitch is thrown
  while the player sees GET READY and nothing else. On the phone the SWING button is
  visible but the same hint is a scroll away.
- There is no guided first at-bat. The first pitch is live.
- The batting controls are three modes (Contact, Power, Bunt) with no on-screen note
  of what each trades. The help explains it (7111-7115); the screen does not.
- Pitching: the 3x3 grid has no labels (high, low, inside, outside). "Pick a pitch." is
  the whole prompt. The batter card says POW CON SPD but not what to throw against them.
  The help promises "Every batter has a hidden weak pitch. Find it", and the game never
  signals when you have found it: `scheduleCpuSwing` 4241 adds jitter silently, and no
  callout, log line or sound says "he cannot hit that".
- HUD jargon with no explanation: "Top 1/5", "Outs 0 · 0-0", "SIDE RETIRED". A first
  timer does not know 0-0 is balls and strikes.
- Between innings: SIDE RETIRED for 1.4s, then three seconds of nothing, then the next
  batter. No "what happens next" (you are pitching now, here is who is up).
- After the game: "You win." or "You lose." then the box score, then one button. Fine as
  a record, no read on what to do next in a season (next opponent, standing).

### Jargon to lighten

| Where | Now | Suggested |
|---|---|---|
| HUD 6657 | `Outs 0 · 0-0` | `0 out · 0 balls 0 strikes` on desktop; keep the short form on phones with a one-time tooltip |
| HUD 6654 | `Top 1/5` | `Inning 1 of 5 · they bat` / `you bat` |
| callout 5153 | `SIDE RETIRED` | `3 OUTS · SWITCH SIDES` |
| hub 3289 | `Free-swingers`, `Small ball` | keep, add a one-line gloss on hover or beneath |
| box score 7020 | `AB H HR K SB AVG` | keep for the table, add a legend line |
| season stats 3374 | `Hits 0 · HR 0 · K 0 · SB 0 · AB 0` | `0 hits · 0 homers · 0 strikeouts · 0 steals in 0 at bats` |
| press copy | "nine outs from the flight home" | fine, it is character voice |

## B. Scene and situation coherence

### Transitions

- Every screen has a way forward and back. No dead ends found in the router (1760).
- Abandoning a game: `End Game` (6764) in a season returns to the hub without recording
  anything, and `Play Next Game` offers the same opponent again. This is a free re-roll
  of any game that is going badly. The header Main Menu button (1777) does the same
  through a confirm. Either record a forfeit loss or say plainly that the game is
  abandoned and will be replayed.
- A season save from before franchises existed loads (fixed this morning, boot at 7247).
- `renderResult` (6953) for a playoff or cup game returns to the bracket; the bracket then
  offers the next action. Good.
- Season end: both buttons on `renderSeasonEnd` (7228-7235) delete the save. There is no
  way to look at the finished season again after that screen.

### Baseball edge cases

- **Walk-off walk does not end the game.** `scoreRuns` (5140) sets `g.walkoff` and fires
  the WALK-OFF plaque. `recordWalk` (5083) then calls `nextBatter` and `startAtBat`,
  which checks only `g.over` (3798). Reproduced headless: bottom 5, tied, bases loaded,
  two outs, walk. Result: home 4, away 3, `over: false`, next batter at the plate.
  The same path is used by Phoenix's rebirth (5111). Hits and errors go through
  `checkWalkOff` (4661) or `afterHitTransition` (5024) and end correctly.
- **Extra innings** are unbounded and unannounced. `checkGameOver` (5180) continues a tie
  correctly, but there is no EXTRA INNINGS callout, the HUD reads "Top 6/5", and the
  in-game line score draws columns only for `g.innings` (6816), so the extra frames are
  not on the board until the result screen (6995 handles it).
- **Mercy rule**: 15 runs, from inning `max(3, innings-1)` (5204-5210). The comment says
  25. Nobody is told. The game just ends and the result screen says You win.
- **Walk-off on a hit** works (4661). Squeeze bunt goes through the same finish (4661).
  Good.
- **Pinch hitters, substitutions, bullpen**: none exist. The first pick pitches every
  inning of every game (`currentPitcher` 3630). Fatigue is per game, so a nine inning
  game with one pitcher is a design choice, but the player is not told there is no
  bullpen.
- **Standings ties**: the hub sorts wins then losses (3339); seeding sorts wins then
  `Math.random()` (3428). With 18 clubs over 7 games ties at the fourth seed are common.
  Simulation of the game's own league model: 5-2 makes the bracket 87 percent of the
  time, 4-3 makes it 9 percent. The player gets no tiebreak rule, no explanation, and
  the two tables can disagree.
- **Missing the playoffs**: `nextPlayoffMatch` (3527) returns CPU matches, the bracket
  button says "Simulate CPU Match" three times, then "View Season Summary". The hub
  banner says "Playoffs." (3274). Nothing anywhere says the player's season is over.
- **Playoff seeding**: higher seed hosts, stated in help. Good. Only four teams, no byes
  needed.
- **Cup**: the player is always entrant 0 (2980), so `youHome` (3053) is always true:
  you host every cup game including the final. Cup CPU results come from a rating
  formula with a +1 tiebreak (3031-3039). Fine, but never called an upset when it is one.
- **Exhibition**: you are always the visitor (2933 passes no `youHome`), so a walk-off
  can never happen to you in the mode a first-timer plays first.
- **Steals**: only first to second, only during the windup. Caught stealing for the
  third out goes to `endHalfInning` (6940). Good.
- **Double play** with the bases loaded advances nobody and scores nobody (4668-4680).
  With runners on first and third, a real double play usually scores the run from
  third unless it is the third out. Minor.

### Are stat differences felt

Monte Carlo of `resolveSwing`'s in-zone branch (4304-4407), medium difficulty, normal
swing, 200,000 plate appearances per row:

| Batter | Timing | HR | XBH | 1B | Out |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Kong (POW 99, CON 45, SPD 8) | perfect | 22.8 | 14.7 | 24.6 | 37.9 |
| Peter Pan (POW 40, CON 62, SPD 96) | perfect | 14.1 | 11.1 | 44.2 | 30.5 |
| Sherlock (POW 55, CON 92, SPD 38) | perfect | 16.5 | 12.2 | 33.0 | 38.3 |
| Kong | sloppy (0.07) | 6.3 | 10.6 | 15.6 | 67.5 |
| Peter Pan | sloppy | 4.2 | 7.7 | 35.4 | 52.7 |
| Sherlock | sloppy | 6.3 | 9.4 | 25.5 | 58.8 |

- Power is felt: Kong homers half again as often as Peter at the same timing.
- Speed is felt more at the plate than on the bases: SPD adds up to 0.10 to the single
  odds (4399), and the aggressive send (4721) and steal odds (6924) are the only base
  running effects. The player never makes a base running decision beyond Steal 2nd.
- Contact is only felt when timing is off (`contactBoost` 4304, `foulBand` 4311). With
  perfect timing Sherlock and Kong differ mostly by power. That is fine, but the game
  never tells the player CON is a forgiveness stat.
- Defense: widens the throw and catch windows (4820, 4937), lowers error odds (4540),
  raises double play odds (4561). Felt only when the player is fielding, and the
  window change is small: DEF 90 versus 50 moves the green zone from 0.06 to 0.11 of
  the bar.
- At the controls, Power and Speed characters do not FEEL different: same ring, same
  sweep, same swing, same run frames. The difference is in the outcome table, not in
  the hands. The Contact and Power swing modes (4293-4294) move the ring visibly and are
  the one place the player feels a choice.

### Proposed feeders that fit the tone

1. **Player of the game card** on the result screen: the `pressStar` (2266) pick already
   exists, give it a sprite, a line (the character's `PLAYER_LINES` entry) and the
   night's stat line. Cheap, and it makes the box score a story.
2. **Win fanfare**: a short original triad on Sound (one already exists at 1531 for
   another cue), confetti on the result heading, the kit colour behind it. Loss gets a
   quiet aww.
3. **Strikeout counter and K plaque**: when you are pitching, a strikeout shows
   `STRIKEOUT · 4 K` and the fatigue chip area shows the running K count. Nine K's in a
   game gets a signature (the pitcher's own overlay).
4. **Catch and throw plaques promoted**: a green-zone catch reads `CAUGHT · nice` and a
   green throw reads `OUT AT FIRST`, hit style not sub style. A late-inning catch with
   runners on gets the crowd standing.
5. **Milestone toasts** from the per-player season totals that already exist
   (`perPlayer` 5235): first homer with a character, tenth hit, third steal, first
   shutout, tenth win in PROGRESS. A toast is a small plaque at the top of the field.
6. **Hot and cold streaks**: per character hits in last 10 AB, shown as a flame or ice
   chip on the at-bat card and the draft card. Feeds the "who do I lead off" decision.
7. **Season awards** on the season end screen: MVP (your side), Home Run King,
   Speed Demon, Golden Glove (fewest errors, most catches), each with a sprite. Save the
   winners to PROGRESS so a trophy case can show them on the menu.
8. **Rivalry callout**: `pressRival` already tracks history. Put one line on the hub
   next game card ("You have split two with Ironsides") and a RIVALRY GAME plaque at
   first pitch.
9. **Near-miss feedback**: a foul with contact quality above 0.8 or a fly out with
   `dy < -1.35` reads `JUST FOUL` or `WARNING TRACK` instead of FOUL BALL or FLY OUT.
   The trajectory already knows.
10. **Daily character challenge**: seeded by date, "Hit a homer with Pooh today" with a
    plaque on the menu. Progress in PROGRESS, no server. Lives beside the unlocks.
11. **Cosmetic unlocks that show in-game**: a kit trim, a cap, a bat colour earned by a
    title, drawn on the field sprite (the generator already carries headwear specs).

## D. Graphics

### Consistency

- Every character sprite comes from one generator at 32x40 (`V2_W`, `V2_H`; `HERO_W` at
  1423) with six poses (`POSES`, gen_sprites_v2.py:2830) and material-coloured
  outlines. Palette, density and outline style are consistent across all 54 by
  construction, and the build guard rejects duplicate faces.
- Field furniture is drawn with canvas primitives at a different density: the ball is an
  anti-aliased 5px circle (6446), the bat a round-capped stroke (6467), the timing ring
  an anti-aliased ellipse (6565), the bases small diamonds. None of it is pixel-snapped,
  so the sprites read as pixel art on a smooth field.
- Signature overlays use `serif` at four sites (6242, 6272, 6329, 6337) against a
  monospace pixel UI. Off-style.
- The crowd, wall, track and clouds are pixel-blocked (5620-5697). Good.

The two frame run cycle is reused for every movement: fielder chase, runner, batter to
first. Reuse is acceptable; the absent pitcher animation is not, because the pitcher is
the figure the player watches for two seconds every pitch.

### Readability

- Desktop 960x660 canvas: the ball is 5px, which is half a percent of the width. The
  trail (6154) helps. The pitch ball is drawn only until it reaches the plate and the
  batted ball has a ground shadow (6093). Readable but small.
- Phone: the canvas is about 340px wide, so the ball is 2px and the fielders are 10px
  tall. The park placard (6638) sits at top centre and overlaps both score placards at
  390px (screenshot). The four corner chips cover the 1B and 3B fielders.
- The fielder "you control": you do not control one. The chase (5528) picks the nearest
  post automatically and the player times a throw or a catch. That is fine, but the
  catch ring appears at the landing spot with no line from the fielder, so on a phone
  it is not obvious who is catching.
- Runners are readable on desktop. On the phone the on-deck circle and catcher fade with
  the camera (5971), which helps.
- The plaque sits at 38 percent height (170), which is the mound: STRIKE covers the
  pitcher and the incoming ball's release point (screenshot 06).

## E. Gameplay feel

- **Input**: click anywhere (capture phase, buttons excluded, 4171), Space, or the SWING
  button on narrow screens. Responsive. Touch goes through `ontouchstart` on the SWING
  button only; a tap on the field goes through click. Fine.
- **Timing windows**, medium: sweep 2.3s, sweet half-width 0.10 (`DIFF` 3687), so the
  green window is about 460ms, 575ms in Contact, 345ms in Power. Foul band 1.45x to
  1.7x that. A whiff needs an error over about 390ms. First-timers will rarely strike
  out swinging, which is the right onboarding shape. Hard is 1.9s and 0.16.
- **Pitch variety**: seven types, three per pitcher (`PITCH_BOOK` 3661), each with its
  own flight path (6128-6152). Good. Because the ring closes on the pitch's `ideal`
  anyway, the variety is felt as a timing shift and a shape, not as a decision. The
  knuckler's wobble is the one pitch that punishes muscle memory.
- **Difficulty curve across a season**: `opponentSharpness` (898) runs 0 to 0.75 over the
  seven games, scaling meter wobble by up to 1.68x and tightening CPU jitter by 0.04.
  Playoffs 0.85, final 1.0. The curve exists and the hub labels it. It is mild: the
  batting window barely changes, the CPU squares up a little more.
- **CPU behaviour that breaks immersion**:
  - The CPU never bunts, never steals, never uses Contact or Power (4291). Every CPU
    at bat is the same shape.
  - The CPU pitcher grooves the middle on 3-0 55 percent of the time (3862), which is
    fine, but with two strikes it chases corners 35 percent and the player is never
    told a corner pitch might be a ball (the ring does not change).
  - One pitcher per side per game, no bullpen, so a 9-inning CPU pitcher is GASSED from
    the 6th and stays in.
  - A runner thrown out at the plate on a single (4724) happens with no animation: the
    log says it, the plaque says OUT AT HOME, the runner vanishes.
- **Agency gaps**: no send or hold on the bases (the task list says it shipped; the code
  has only `attemptSteal` 6914 and the automatic send at 4721). No fielder choice. No
  pitching change.

## F. Season and cup modes

- **Rising stakes**: the sharpness label on the next game card (3289) and the finale
  and playoff press pools (2021-2040) are the whole arc. Nothing in the standings says
  what a win today does. No "clinched", no "eliminated", no "win and you are in".
- **Standings, schedule, playoff picture**: the hub shows a W-L table (3329), the next
  opponent, and past results. It does not show the remaining schedule, the home and away
  pattern (`homeAt` 2963 exists), a playoff line under fourth, or games back. The
  playoff picture is never visible, and finding 2 above is the consequence.
- **Cup versus season**: structurally different (bracket, one save slot, single
  elimination banner 3079). Tension is stated, not built: no round-by-round sharpness
  line on the cup screen (the season hub has one), no opponent style, no "upset" when a
  low seed wins a CPU tie, and you host every game (3053) so the final never feels like
  an away trip. The cup does not have a franchise, a park or a press media day, so it
  starts colder than the season.
- **Progression between games**: press memory (2140) and rivalry counts (`pressRival`)
  are the only state that carries. No roster changes, no hot or cold form, no injuries
  or fatigue between games, no rewards for a season or cup beyond `PROGRESS.titles` and
  the Dragon unlock. The season end screen (7207) shows a record, a champion name and
  four totals, then both buttons delete the save.
- **Replay value**: 54 characters, 6 parks, 18 opponents, 12 cities, press choices. The
  raw material is there. What is missing is a place it accumulates: a trophy case, a
  career record, best seasons, most used characters.

## Things that are already right and should be protected

- The press room. It is the best thing in the season and the settings copy is honest.
- The timing ring on the field. Watching the ball and watching the ring are one act.
- The chase: fielders converge on the ball, so hit or out reads before the plaque.
- The franchise founding flow and the park factors stated in plain numbers.
- The league advancing in lockstep with the player's games.
- The regression guards: `check-posture.mjs`, the dash check, the sprite face guard.
