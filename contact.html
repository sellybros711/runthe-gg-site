Get in Touch

We'd love to hear from you

RunThe.GG is made by two brothers, and a real person reads every message. There's no support ticket queue and no chatbot, just us. Email us at RunTheGames@outlook.com and we'll get back to you as soon as we can, usually within a couple of days.

What to get in touch about

Found a bug? Tell us which game you were in, what you were doing when it broke, and what device and browser you were using. A screenshot helps enormously. Bugs that cost somebody a run go to the front of the queue.
Account or leaderboard help Can't sign in, lost saved progress, need a username changed, or think a leaderboard entry is wrong? Email from the address on the account if you can. It makes it much faster for us to help.
Delete your account or your data Ask us and we'll remove your account, email, username and saved progress. You don't have to give a reason. See our Privacy Policy for exactly what we hold.
Ideas and feedback Want a sport we don't cover, a mode we haven't built, or a rule changed? A lot of what's on the site started as somebody's email. We read all of them, even when we can't build them.
Advertising and partnerships For media enquiries, sponsorship or anything commercial, email the same address with "Partnership" in the subject line and it'll reach the right one of us.
Rights holders and press Our games are unofficial fan projects (see our Disclaimer). If you represent a league, club, athlete or data provider and have a concern about anything on the site, email us and we'll respond quickly.

Reach us directly at RunTheGames@outlook.com
