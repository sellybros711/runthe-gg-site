About RunThe.GG

Run The Games: data driven simulations

RunThe.GG is home to Run The Games: quick, free, data-driven sports simulations you play right in your browser. There is nothing to download and nothing to install. Open the page and play in seconds on your phone, tablet, or computer.

What we build

Every RunThe.GG game is a simulation, not an arcade game. Under the hood, real statistics and real match logic drive the results, so outcomes feel earned rather than random. You make the decisions, who to draft, how to build your player or squad, when to push for a win, and the engine plays them out with realistic ranges. No two runs are ever quite the same.

Play instantly, keep your progress

You never need an account to play. Every game is free and loads straight in your browser, with no sign-up wall in front of it. If you want your progress to stick, make one free account and your trophies, streaks, saved careers, and leaderboard rank follow you across every game on the site and across all of your devices.

The games

RunTheTour is a 30-year golf career simulation: draft a golfer one skill at a time, then guide their whole career through majors, world rankings, rivalries, and an eventual run at the Hall of Fame.

The Perfect Season is an NFL roster draft game: spin a year and a team, sign six all-time legends under a $140M salary cap, then play all 17 games and chase a perfect season. Every player is priced and scored from his real statistics. There is a One Franchise mode where you lock the wheel to one franchise, build the best team in its history, and compete on that franchise's leaderboard.

RunThePitch is a World Cup squad builder: spin a nation and a year, draft a legend each round, assemble your dream squad, then simulate your run, on your own, in the daily challenge, or live head-to-head against other players.

More free sports games are in development, all under the same single login.

Who makes RunThe.GG

We're two brothers who love sports and love building games. RunThe.GG is our project to make simple, honest, free-to-play games and share them with anyone who wants to play. Have feedback or an idea for a game? We'd love to hear it: reach us any time on our contact page.
