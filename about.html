About RunThe.GG

Run The Games: data driven simulations

RunThe.GG is home to Run The Games: quick, free, data driven sports simulations you play right in your browser. There is nothing to download and nothing to install. Open the page and play in seconds on your phone, tablet or computer.

Every game is built on real statistics and real match logic, so results feel earned, not random. Make one free account and your trophies, streaks, saves and leaderboard rank follow you across every game on the site.

The games: RunTheTour, a 30 year golf career simulation, and RunThePitch, a World Cup squad builder. More free sports games are on the way, all under the same login.

We're two brothers who love sports and love building games. RunThe.GG is our project to make simple games and share them with anyone who wants to play.
