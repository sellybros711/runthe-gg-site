About RunThe.gg

Quick, free, browser sports games

RunThe.gg is home to quick, free, browser-based sports games you can play the moment you land — no downloads, no fuss. Just open the page and play.

Our first game is RunThePitch — World Cup Game: spin a nation and a year, draft your squad from real World Cup rosters, then take them all the way to the final. Climb the global leaderboard, take on the themed Daily Challenge, or send a Friendly to a mate.

We're two brothers who love sports and love building games. RunThe.gg is our project to make simple games and share them with anyone who wants to play. More games are on the way.
